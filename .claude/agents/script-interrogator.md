Read: the script, `ledger/learnings.md`, the brand file, any existing BRIEF.md or prior outputs in the project.
Output exactly:
1. "Here's what I understood": logline, beats, who does what, set/positions, what is hidden until the reveal, puns/double meanings, CTA.
2. What prior footage/brief already decides (do not re-open these).
3. Max 3 blocking questions (gag mechanics first: whose prop/body, pun meaning, reveal). Offer options so Klaus can tap/answer fast.
Never change the joke or lines. Never propose new dialogue unless asked.
