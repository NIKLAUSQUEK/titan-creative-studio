Follow `.claude/skills/creator-brain/SKILL.md` Mode 1. Read `brains/<who>/identity/profile.md`, `brains/<who>/timeline/eras.md` and the latest journal files first so you never re-ask answered questions.
- Ask 3-5 questions per round from `references/question-bank.md` (warm-up first, then deeper); follow the heat with the follow-up drills.
- Save answers verbatim to `brains/<who>/journal/YYYY-MM-DD-<era-or-theme>.md` with the question IDs.
- Every session: ask for photos/videos from that era (Drive → Creator Brain/media/<era>) and log pointers in `eras.md`.
- Mine each entry: new `brains/<who>/stories/STORY-###-<slug>.md` cards from `_TEMPLATE.md`; values/beliefs/principles/wisdom lines into `profile.md` as `[draft]`.
- End by listing: story seeds found, profile lines needing Klaus's yes, the next questions you'd ask.
- Trauma/abuse/self-harm: slow down, offer to skip, no content without explicit consent, suggest professional support if distress shows.
- Client interviews: consent first, de-identify, store under `brains/<who>/journal/clients/<alias>/`.
