# Question bank
Ask 3-5 per session: one warm-up, then deeper. IDs let the journal record what was asked. Always end a session with a media ask and "What did I not ask that I should have?"

## Origin (O)
- O-01 What's the story of how your parents met, as it was told at home? What did they leave out?
- O-02 What was expected of you before you could even talk?
- O-03 Which language did love sound like at home? Which one did scolding sound like?
- O-04 What did your family believe about men, money, health and success? Which do you still carry?

## Childhood (C)
- C-01 Your earliest memory. Where are you standing, what can you smell?
- C-02 Who did you want to be at 8, and who told you that you couldn't?
- C-03 The first time you felt ashamed of your body or who you were.
- C-04 The first time you won something and nobody noticed.
- C-05 A rule at home you broke. What happened?

## Teens (T)
- T-01 Where did you sit in the canteen, and why there?
- T-02 What did you pretend to be good at?
- T-03 First heartbreak: what did you tell yourself afterwards that wasn't true?
- T-04 Which adult saw something in you before you did?
- T-05 What were you most insecure about, and what do you think now when you see a teenager with the same insecurity?

## Young adult (Y)
- Y-01 NS / uni / first job: the moment you realised the world doesn't care about your plans.
- Y-02 Why physiotherapy? Give me the real reason, not the interview answer.
- Y-03 A mentor's sentence you still hear in your head.
- Y-04 A relationship that changed how you see yourself.
- Y-05 Your first real failure. Who did you call?

## Builder (B)
- B-01 The day you decided to start your own thing. What were you scared of?
- B-02 The patient who changed how you practise (de-identified).
- B-03 The worst month in the business. What kept you going?
- B-04 A win that felt empty. A loss that turned out to be a gift.
- B-05 What do men refuse to say out loud in your clinic, and what does that cost them?
- B-06 What would you tell the version of you who started?

## Future self (F)
- F-01 What do you want people to say about you when you're not in the room?
- F-02 What are you building that will outlast you?

## Themes (use with any era)
- **Identity (ID):** What part of you did you hide to fit in? What do you refuse to change about yourself now?
- **Insecurity (INS):** What compliment can't you accept? What do you check in the mirror first?
- **Barriers (BAR):** What did you believe you couldn't do because of where you came from?
- **Growth (G):** What used to scare you that now bores you? What habit rebuilt you?
- **Wins (W):** The win you're proudest of that would look small to anyone else.
- **Losses (L):** What did you lose that you're grateful for now?
- **Lessons (LS):** What do you know now that you'd have paid anything to know at 20?
- **Love (LV):** What did a breakup teach you about yourself, not about them?
- **Body (BD):** When did your body stop being a project and start being a home, if it has?
- **Money (M):** First money you earned; what you bought; what it taught you.
- **Faith / beliefs (FB):** What do you believe that most people around you don't?
- **Random (R):** A stranger who said something you never forgot. A smell that takes you back. A song tied to a turning point. The dumbest thing you did that turned out right.

## Follow-up drills (when an answer has heat)
"What happened next?" · "What did you feel in your body?" · "What did you decide about yourself that day?" · "Who else was there?" · "If you could say one sentence to them now?" · "Is that the whole truth?"
