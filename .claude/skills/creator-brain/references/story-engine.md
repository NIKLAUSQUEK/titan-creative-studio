# Story engine

## 1. Goal → what the piece must make the audience feel
| Goal | Audience should feel | Lean on | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wise | "he's been where I am and came out clearer" | personal story, one hard-won lesson, quiet wisdom line | lecturing, lists |
| Expert / knowledgeable | "I finally understand" | myth vs fact, mechanism explained simply, one surprising number (sourced) | jargon, hedging everything |
| Trust | "he's honest with me" | admitting a mistake, showing the unglamorous part, nuance | hype, superlatives |
| Sell | "this solves my exact problem, and it's safe to act" | problem story → stakes → simple next step | testimonials/discounts/superlatives (SG health rules) |
| Founder story | "I'm part of something with a reason" | origin wound → mission → proof → invitation | humble-brag, rags-to-riches clichés |
| Entertain | laugh, then think | skit, irony, a reveal | punching down |

## 2. Trope stances (pick one per piece)
- **Agree and deepen:** the trope is right, here's the part nobody says.
- **Challenge:** the trope is wrong, and here's evidence/story.
- **Subvert:** start as the trope, then flip the prize (STORY-001: revenge body → the real gain is who you became).
- **Flip the hero:** tell it from the other person's side (the ex, the patient, the parent).
- **Both true:** hold two opposites ("Discipline matters. So does rest.").
Common tropes to play with: revenge body, hustle 24/7, rags to riches, "real men don't…", the one who got away, follow your passion, money can't buy happiness, the comeback, the mentor who saves you, overnight success, "it's just genetics".

## 3. Hook formulas (write 5, rank them)
1. **Contrarian:** "Stop doing X. It's why Y." / "X is a lie we tell men."
2. **Confession:** "I've never told anyone this, but…"
3. **Cold open in the scene:** drop into a specific moment, sensory detail first.
4. **Curiosity gap:** "Nobody tells you what happens after…"
5. **Bold outcome + twist:** "I got the six-pack. She left anyway."
6. **Question that stings:** "When did you last cry, and who saw it?"
7. **Number + surprise:** "1 in 3 men… and most will never say it." (sourced)
8. **Pattern interrupt:** visual or sound oddity in the first second (studio handles it).
Rules: first 2 s / ~12 words, a person or a tension, no throat-clearing ("Hey guys…").

## 4. Structures
- **Short (15-60 s):** Hook → context in one line → the turn (by ~60%) → lesson → wisdom line → soft CTA.
- **Story-lesson (60-120 s):** Scene → want → obstacle → low point → realisation → lesson → wisdom note → "so here's what I'd do".
- **Myth-buster (expert):** Myth → why people believe it → what's actually true (mechanism) → what to do → callback.
- **Founder arc:** Before (wound) → the moment → the decision → the cost → proof it matters → invitation.
- **Long form (5-20 min / essay):** Cold open → stakes → 3 escalating beats (each with a mini-lesson) → lowest point → turn → the big lesson → wisdom note → callback to the opening image.
- **Parable / fiction:** Everyman character → relatable want → trope-driven plan → the plan "works" but the prize is empty → the real prize → principle. Label it fiction.
- **Carousel:** slide 1 hook, 2 the stakes, 3-6 one idea per slide, 7 the wisdom note, 8 CTA.

## 5. Emotive language by scene
- **Shame / insecurity:** small, physical, quiet ("I wore the hoodie in 32 degrees").
- **Grief / loss:** absence of an object ("her toothbrush was still in the cup").
- **Anger / drive:** short sentences. Verbs. Time stamps ("5:12 a.m. Again.").
- **Hope / turn:** light and space ("the car park was empty, and for once that felt fine").
- **Wisdom:** plain words, one breath, no adjectives.
Show, don't name, the emotion. One sensory detail per scene. Cut every "very", "really", "literally".

## 6. Short vs long form
- Short: one story, one lesson, one feeling. Cut backstory to a single line.
- Long: room for nuance, contradiction, a second story that complicates the first, research, and the "both true" stance.

## 7. Repurpose ladder (one story → many pieces)
Long video/podcast → 3-5 shorts (each a different hook) → carousel (lessons) → quote cards (wisdom notes) → caption/thread → newsletter. See `clipping-podcast.md` for the cutting workflow.

## 8. Final checks
True to profile.md · one lesson · sensory detail · earned emotion · contrarian not cruel · fiction labelled · client consent · SG health rules for sell pieces · CTA matches goal.
