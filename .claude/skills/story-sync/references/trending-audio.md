# Trending audio

## Lifecycle
- Publicis/TikTok study (2026, Australia): median trend lifespan **3 days**; 55.9% die within a work week; 27% last > 2 weeks (AdNews).
- Practitioner guides: most trends peak 7-10 days after breaking; **formats outlive sounds**; some sounds run for months; trends differ by country.
- Instagram's trending arrow marks a *rising* sound in Reels; original audio and old covers can trend months later [P].
**Jump on** only if you can ship in 24-72 h, it is rising (not peaking), and it fits the niche. Otherwise borrow the **format**, not the sound.

## Licensing (who may use what)
| Account | Allowed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brand / business on TikTok | **TikTok Commercial Music Library** (600k+ pre-cleared tracks; Business Account; filter by region, placement, mood, duration) or original | Using the general library to promote a business risks infringement [O]. CML clearance reported as TikTok-only (vendor) - re-license for IG/YouTube |
| Brand / business on Instagram/Facebook | **Meta Sound Collection** (royalty-free) or licensed/original | Many business accounts can't access the licensed library; saving a song from a personal account doesn't get around it |
| YouTube | YouTube Audio Library (check per-track terms; some CC tracks need attribution) or licensed/original | uses beyond YouTube (ads, client work) need explicit licences |
| Personal / creator (Klaus) | trending chart sounds **added in-app at posting**, never baked into the exported file | record the exact sound + link in PUBLISH.md (ledger) |
Brands can't use chart music because personal-library licences cover non-commercial UGC, not advertising; commercial use needs sync + master licences.

## Adapting a trend with licensed audio
1. Break the trend into its beat map: where the hook, the "switch" and the reveal fall (which bar).
2. Find a CML / Sound Collection / library track within ±5 BPM with the drop in the same place (`/sound` → Higgsfield `tiktok_music_trending` by country).
3. Rebuild the visual template: same transitions on the same beats.
4. Put the brand twist on the "switch".
5. Localise text and voice (Singlish/Malay/Mandarin/Vietnamese per localisation.md); regional CML filters.

## Own the trend
Create an original, reusable audio hook - a branded line, jingle, voice meme or sonic logo - that others can lip-sync or reuse. Fully licensed, and doubles as a distinctive brand asset (marketing-effectiveness.md).
