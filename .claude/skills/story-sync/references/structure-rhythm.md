# Story structure → edit rhythm

| Structure | Shot-length curve | B-roll placement | Music arc | SFX punctuation | Silence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook → problem → solution → CTA | fast (≤ 1.5 s) → slower problem (2-3 s) → brisk solution → hold CTA | problem: literal pain B-roll; solution: product macro / process proof | tense/minor bed → lift to major at the solution | hit on hook; whoosh into solution; ding on CTA | 0.5 s before the solution reveal |
| Man-in-a-hole (pain → relief) | medium → slower in the hole → quicker climb | relatable struggle → process | minor → building major | riser out of the hole | at the lowest point |
| Storytime / 5-second moment | mostly A-roll, cuts follow speech | sparse illustrative + reaction inserts at peaks | low bed; swell at the turn | freeze / record-scratch at "and then…" | before the twist line |
| Myth-buster | myth fast and mocking → truth slower, authoritative | myth: recreated hack/graphic; truth: real demo, data | quirky → clean/confident | buzzer on the myth, pop on the fact | before "here's the truth" |
| Before / after (behavioural, never clinical results) | before: longer, flatter, desaturated; after: brighter, faster | matched angles (match cut) | minor → major lift | whoosh/riser at the switch | - |
| Listicle / open-loop list | steady, metric; equal per item | one B-roll cluster per item | constant loop, small accent per item | pop/number-card per item | - |
| Day-in-the-life | rhythmic 0.7-2 s with time stamps | B-roll heavy (70%+) | light lo-fi/acoustic loop | natural foley (coffee, keys) as ASMR | - |

| Moral skit | 2-4 s coverage | reaction CU after every key line | sad/tense → sting + silence at reveal → uplifting swell | sting at reveal | at the reveal |
| Kishōtenketsu (festive, no villain) | gentle, even | everyday detail, place | warm, consonant; small lift at the twist (Ten) | almost none | at the twist |
| Manifesto | slow → accelerating crescendo → one held final image | metaphor/effort B-roll cut to VO cadence | solo instrument → full; drop at the last line | ≤ 3 impacts on key words | before the final line |
| Product hero | 1-2 s macro beats, 3-5 s hero hold | product is the picture | beat-synced; silence for the hero hold | riser → impact on logo | before the hero hold |
| Explainer (map/motion/clean) | 2-6 s, chapters | graphic per named concept/number | curious bed → tension at twist → resolve | pops on labels | before the twist |
| Episodic / cliffhanger | per format | per format | unresolved at the end (no button) | riser into the cut-off | none - cut on the question |

## Retention curve map (short form)
0-3 s hook · 3-8 s context + first "but" · 30-40% escalation or first payoff (prevents the mid-sag) · 60-75% lowest point / reversal · 80-90% payoff · last 10% loop or CTA. Read every analytics dip against the beat at that timestamp, then `/learn`.
