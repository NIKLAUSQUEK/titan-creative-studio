# Style sync patterns (per editor preset)

How each researched style places B-roll, fillers and music against emotion. Generated from the `sync` block of `tools/editor/presets/*.json` (2026-10-25 research; values marked est. in the sources are estimates from public breakdowns - calibrate with measured references). Creator-level evidence: `research/sources-style-library.md`. Pick the preset whose pattern matches the Sync Map, then override per beat.

## clean-explainer — Clean explainer (Ali Abdaal-style)
Calm curious talking head, kinetic text beside the speaker with yellow keywords, framework cards per point, paper transitions, low lo-fi bed.

- **B-roll:** screen recordings, book covers, simple diagrams on each named concept or number; ~65:35; mostly PiP beside the speaker, full-frame diagrams 2-4 s
- **Fillers:** text card per framework point, push-in on the key line, paper transition between sections (every 30-60 s)
- **Music:** one low lo-fi bed, ducked hard (-22..-24 dB), stops for emphasis lines; no beat-cutting
- **Emotion map:** curiosity → clarity → relatability → empowerment (music lifts slightly)
- **Transitions:** hard cuts in sections, paper fold/tear between sections
- **Beats:** curiosity question / reframe 0-5s → promise 5-15s → 3 points: card → example each → recap + soft CTA
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -23 dB · SFX 0.8/10 s · zoom every 10 s

## luxury-broll — Luxury B-roll talking head (Iman Gadzhi-style)
Cinematic two-angle talking head over aspirational B-roll, accent-colour UI/graph pops, animation-dense intro (4-10 visuals/min) easing to ~2/min.

- **B-roll:** metaphor/aspirational B-roll on abstract nouns ('freedom', 'scale'), archival self-footage on story beats; ~50:50; full-frame 1.5-3 s
- **Fillers:** reused chapter-card animation, UI/graph pops, angle switch on key phrases; every 4-8 s
- **Music:** one moody bed per chapter, hits synced to chapter cards, B-roll montages on the beat, voice dominant
- **Emotion map:** aspiration → credibility → vulnerability (music thins) → resolve → desire
- **Transitions:** cut on phrase, whoosh/blur into B-roll, light leaks
- **Beats:** proof/claim hook 0-3s → open loop, dense visuals 3-15s → chapters: claim → B-roll → graph card → flashback / vulnerability → lesson → CTA
- **Mix defaults:** music mid @ -20 dB · SFX 1.5/10 s · zoom every 6 s

## magic-transition — Magic transition one-take (Zach King-style)
Single-take feel; a mundane setup transforms through a hidden cut (whip, lens block, object pass); SFX hit sells the magic; loopable end. Sound-off friendly.

- **B-roll:** none: one continuous A-roll
- **Fillers:** none; the illusion is the interrupt; hold 0.3-0.5 s after the reveal
- **Music:** one light track; the transformation lands on a beat + SFX; brief silence just before the reveal
- **Emotion map:** familiarity → anticipation → surprise/delight → loop urge
- **Transitions:** hidden only: whip pan, body/object pass across lens, match on action
- **Beats:** mundane setup 0-2s → misdirection gesture → hidden cut / composite → reveal + SFX hit → deadpan hold / loop
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -22 dB · SFX 0.8/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## moral-skit — Moral-lesson skit (Dhar Mann-style)
Scripted archetype drama: injustice hook → escalating wrongs → reveal → consequence → moral stated to camera. Make 4-5 hook/ending variants.

- **B-roll:** establishing exteriors and prop inserts (letters, phones) on plot-critical lines; ~90:10
- **Fillers:** reaction close-up after every key line, slow push-in on realisation, flashback cut
- **Music:** score per act: sad/tense during wrongs → silence or sting at the reveal → uplifting swell at the consequence/moral
- **Emotion map:** indignation → rising anger → surprise (sting + silence) → catharsis (swell) → moral warmth
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, dip-to-black / time-jump cards between acts
- **Beats:** injustice hook 0-5s → escalation (2-3 wrongs) → reveal / turn → consequence + apology → moral line + end card
- **Mix defaults:** music mid @ -19 dB · SFX 0.5/10 s · zoom every 15 s

## podcast-clip — Podcast clip (DOAC / clip-channel style)
Hook claim first (no welcome), speaker-fill ↔ stacked split switching, bold karaoke captions, host reaction cutaways, low tension bed, 30-90 s.

- **B-roll:** sparse literal/archival insert on named people, places, stats; ~90:10; 1-2 s
- **Fillers:** host reaction (split or 1 s insert) after big lines, 110-120% punch-in on emphasis, every 5-10 s
- **Music:** low tension bed under the hook, cut on the punchline; voice ≥ 8 dB above
- **Emotion map:** provocation → curiosity → conviction → validation/laugh → reflection
- **Transitions:** layout change (fill ↔ split) acts as the transition
- **Beats:** strongest claim/question 0-3s → who/context 3-8s → argument/story with reaction cuts → punchline / resolution → loop or 'full episode' CTA
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -24 dB · SFX 0.5/10 s · zoom every 7 s

## retention-max — Retention-max (MrBeast-style)
Promise-matched cold open, stakes in 5 s, stair-step escalation with re-engagement spikes, abrupt end. 2024 style lets scenes breathe (~23 cuts/min long-form).

- **B-roll:** scale wides, drone, prize/number inserts on every stake or number word, 0.5-2 s, ~70:30 A:B, full-frame
- **Fillers:** reaction cutaways every few cuts, snap zooms on punchlines, timer/number cards; interrupt every 3-5 s (Shorts)
- **Music:** continuous upbeat cue that switches per segment; 0.5-1 s drop-out before each reveal then impact; montages on the beat
- **Emotion map:** curiosity → awe → investment → tension (ticking, drones) → spike (riser+hit) → triumph → abrupt cut
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, whip/zoom between segments, 'Day 3' time-skip cards
- **Beats:** show the thumbnail promise 0-3s → stakes + rules 3-10s → early wow by ~30s (Shorts: by 5s) → escalate every segment; spike ~every 15s (Shorts) / ~3:00 & ~6:00 (long) → payoff, hard cut, no outro
- **Mix defaults:** music high @ -18 dB · SFX 3/10 s · zoom every 3 s

## silent-deadpan — Silent deadpan fix (Khaby Lame-style)
Over-complicated 'hack' (recreated with our own cast) → static deadpan stare → the obvious simple way → long hold → palms-up gesture. No speech, no captions.

- **B-roll:** the source hack is the 'B' (~40:60 hack:reaction)
- **Fillers:** none; the silent hold is the joke
- **Music:** none or very low; silence carries the punchline
- **Emotion map:** disbelief → anticipation → release → shared smugness
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only
- **Beats:** over-complicated hack 0-6s → cut to deadpan stare → simple fix → 2-3 s hold → gesture / end
- **Mix defaults:** music none @ -24 dB · SFX 0/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## story-vlog — Story vlog / serial challenge (Ryan Trahan-style)
Earnest serialized challenge diary: one-line rules, named recurring segments, running counter, a heart moment, cliffhanger to the next episode.

- **B-roll:** establishing shots between locations, time-lapse on 'later that day', receipts/screens as PiP; ~80:20
- **Fillers:** comedic cutaway that proves/contradicts the line every 20-40 s, freeze + label on introductions, counter graphics
- **Music:** soft bed under talk, cut out for jokes and heartfelt lines, swell on the win, montages on the beat
- **Emotion map:** amusement → curiosity → anxiety (music drops, ambience only) → warmth (acoustic swell) → pride → anticipation
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, location title cards, time-lapse bridges
- **Beats:** tease + one-line challenge 0-8s → recap / counter → named segments + obstacle + stranger/heart moment → setback → small win, counter update → reflection + next-episode cliffhanger
- **Mix defaults:** music mid @ -20 dB · SFX 1/10 s · zoom every 8 s

## storytime-text — Storytime over satisfying B-roll (faceless split)
Original story (never scraped posts) told by VO over looping brand-owned satisfying B-roll, word-by-word captions on the seam, verdict question or Part 2 ending.

- **B-roll:** brand-owned satisfying loop (taping, protein pour, gym) fills one half for the whole piece
- **Fillers:** caption pops, 'Part 1' / 'wait for it' cards
- **Music:** none or very low so the VO dominates
- **Emotion map:** intrigue → indignation → suspense → shock → judgement urge
- **Transitions:** none; cut the series at a cliffhanger
- **Beats:** most dramatic line 0-3s → setup 3-15s → escalation 15-40s → twist 40-50s → verdict question / Part 2 50-60s
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -24 dB · SFX 0.3/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## street-interview — Street interview / vox pop (Daniel Mac, Subway Takes)
One fixed recurring question in 0-2 s, persistent top title bar with the question/take, natural ambience, answer as the payoff, host reaction.

- **B-roll:** rare detail inserts on the reveal (0.5-1 s); ~95:5
- **Fillers:** punch-in on the surprising answer, host reaction insert
- **Music:** none; natural street/train sound carries it
- **Emotion map:** curiosity → surprise/judgement → debate → amusement
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only; the title bar persists (hook_text dur = whole video)
- **Beats:** fixed question 0-2s → answer reveal 2-6s → one follow-up → justification / pushback → host reaction
- **Mix defaults:** music none @ -24 dB · SFX 0.3/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## absurd-sketch — Absurd brand sketch (Liquid Death-style)
Straight-faced genre parody that escalates into absurdity, deadpan tag, heavy sting. Never for clinics.

- **B-roll:** genre-parody inserts played straight
- **Fillers:** reaction holds, crash zoom on the absurd turn
- **Music:** genre-pastiche score played straight, hard stop on the punchline, metal/brand sting on the tag
- **Emotion map:** familiarity → confusion → absurd delight → laugh
- **Transitions:** hard cuts
- **Beats:** straight set-up 0-4s → escalation → punchline (hold) → deadpan tag / loop
- **Mix defaults:** music high @ -16 dB · SFX 2/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## b2b-founder — B2B founder talking head (LinkedIn)
Professional talking head: persistent top headline bar, burned captions with accent highlight, punch-in jump cuts, data callouts, name lower third, soft CTA.

- **B-roll:** screen recordings, data charts, office/process on each insight; 20-30%
- **Fillers:** punch-ins on numbers, number cards per insight
- **Music:** very low or none; sound-off first (LinkedIn autoplays muted)
- **Emotion map:** challenge → recognition → insight → confidence
- **Transitions:** hard cuts; the headline bar persists
- **Beats:** contrarian / numeric hook 0-3s → context / problem → 2-3 numbered insights with callouts → proof / example → takeaway + soft CTA
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -24 dB · SFX 0.5/10 s · zoom every 5 s

## cinematic-vlog — Cinematic vlog (Peter McKinnon / Casey Neistat)
Host-led vlog with cinematic B-roll: sensory cold open, speed ramps, whips, match cuts, jump-cut talking, time-lapse bridges, sincere long-take monologue.

- **B-roll:** sensory macro (coffee, gear, city) at section openers; speed-ramped transitions; ~50%
- **Fillers:** whips, match cuts, time-lapses as bridges
- **Music:** montages cut on downbeats; music out for the sincere monologue (L-cut ambience)
- **Emotion map:** sensation → energy → curiosity → sincerity → satisfaction
- **Transitions:** whip pans with whoosh, match cuts, speed ramps
- **Beats:** sensory B-roll cold open 0-5s → host hook to camera → segments bridged by B-roll montages → sincere monologue (long take) → payoff montage + sign-off
- **Mix defaults:** music mid @ -18 dB · SFX 3/10 s · zoom every 5 s

## clinic-trust — Clinic trust film (premium healthcare)
Calm clinician-led brand film: real staff, soft daylight, slow moves, dissolves, low music, factual supers. SG health-compliant by default.

- **B-roll:** real clinic details: hands, equipment, space, process (never patients' faces/outcomes); 50-70%; 3-5 s holds
- **Fillers:** slow push-ins and dissolves only
- **Music:** felt piano / soft strings, consonant, no drops; ducked under the clinician
- **Emotion map:** recognition → reassurance → trust → calm agency
- **Transitions:** soft dissolves, J-cuts into the clinician
- **Beats:** everyday human moment 0-5s → meet clinicians / approach → care-process B-roll → reassurance line (clinician to camera) → logo + factual info
- **Mix defaults:** music low @ -23 dB · SFX 0/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## doc-trailer — Documentary trailer (Netflix true-story style)
Three-act trailer: bite over black, title cards, accelerating montage on risers, silence, button line, title + date.

- **B-roll:** archival, interviews, establishing; shot length shrinks act by act
- **Fillers:** title cards as act breaks, flash cuts in act 3 (no repeated white flashes)
- **Music:** ambient → pulse → riser-driven climax → hard silence → button sting
- **Emotion map:** intrigue → unease → dread/urgency → shock (silence) → must-watch
- **Transitions:** cuts to black, hard cuts on hits
- **Beats:** cold-open bite over black 0-8s → act 1 setup → act 2 complication → act 3 accelerating montage → silence + button line → title / date card
- **Mix defaults:** music high @ -18 dB · SFX 2/10 s · zoom every 4 s

## manifesto-montage — Manifesto montage (Nike / Gymshark brand film)
VO manifesto over documentary/athlete montage, building from solo instrument to full, ending on a held thesis image and tagline card.

- **B-roll:** metaphor and real-effort footage cut to the VO cadence; 60-90%
- **Fillers:** match cuts, speed ramps on effort peaks (sparingly)
- **Music:** solo instrument → full build; impacts on ≤ 3 key words; drop to silence before the final line
- **Emotion map:** defiance/recognition → struggle → rising pride → conviction (silence) → belonging (tagline)
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat, one held final image
- **Beats:** provocation line + close-up 0-5s → everyday struggle montage → escalation montage → thesis (on camera or silence) → tagline card
- **Mix defaults:** music high @ -18 dB · SFX 1/10 s · zoom every 0 s

## map-explainer — Map/archival explainer (Johnny Harris / Vox-style)

- **B-roll:** maps, archival, documents, data graphics on every place/date/number; 50-70%

- **Music:** low curious bed (plucks/marimba) under setup, tension pulse at the twist, resolve under synthesis
- **Emotion map:** curiosity → understanding → surprise (twist) → insight
- **Transitions:** map zoom-throughs, texture wipes, J-cuts into chapters
- **Beats:** cold-open question 0-15s → title sting → context chapters → twist / scale escalation → synthesis + CTA

## mascot-unhinged — Unhinged mascot / brand-as-creator (Duolingo, Scrub Daddy)

- **Fillers:** meme-style cutaways, zoom on the mascot face, sound-only gag beats every 2-4 s
- **Music:** the trend's audio carries the piece: brand accounts = TikTok CML / Meta Sound Collection track with the same beat map; personal accounts add the trending sound in-app
- **Emotion map:** confusion → amusement → ironic wink → laugh
- **Transitions:** jump cuts on the beat
- **Beats:** meme caption premise 0-2s → escalation → drop / punchline → loop or reply bait

## product-hero — Product hero (Apple-style)
Premium reveal with no talking head: macro edge-light, kinetic claim type on the beat, silence before the hero hold, logo end card.

- **B-roll:** the product IS the picture: macro edges, materials, exploded/CG hybrid; 60-90% of the film
- **Fillers:** kinetic type cards between macro beats; no memes
- **Music:** beat-synced cuts on downbeats; drop to silence for the hero hold, then the sting
- **Emotion map:** intrigue → desire → confidence → awe (silence) → resolve (logo)
- **Transitions:** motion-control moves, match cuts on shape
- **Beats:** macro tease 0-3s → claim type card 3-5s → 3 feature beats (macro + type) → silence / hero hold → end card

## doctor-reacts — Clinician reacts to a viral claim
Recreated claim card or in-app stitch, freeze-frame verdict, diagram explanation, what's actually true, takeaway.

- **B-roll:** claim clip/card ~40%; anatomy/study graphics on 'here's what actually happens'
- **Fillers:** freeze-frame + pause SFX on the claim, punch-in for the verdict, MYTH/FACT cards every 10-20 s
- **Music:** upbeat intro → silence on the freeze/verdict → low bed under explanation → back at the outro
- **Emotion map:** disbelief → humour → curiosity → reassurance → trust
- **Transitions:** freeze-frame as act break, whoosh into graphics
- **Beats:** claim card / stitch 0-3s → freeze + reaction verdict → mechanism with diagram → what's actually true → takeaway

## exercise-demo — Exercise demo education (Prehab Guys / Kayla Itsines)
The exercise is the A-roll: problem title over a moving demo, cue overlays, mistake vs correct, progression, save CTA + safety line.

- **B-roll:** anatomy/arrow overlay on 'this targets…' (PiP, 2-3 s); ~90:10
- **Fillers:** cue text change every 3-5 s, red X / green check for mistake vs correct
- **Music:** steady mid-tempo bed, cut loosely on the beat at angle/exercise changes, no drops
- **Emotion map:** recognition → confidence → 'aha' → motivation
- **Transitions:** hard cuts, split-screen front/side or wrong/right
- **Beats:** problem/target title over demo 0-2s → clean demo reps → cues + common mistake → progression / regression → save CTA + safety line

## fast-recipe — Fast recipe ASMR (Nick DiGiovanni / Khairul Aming)
Hero shot + premise in 1 s, beat-cut ASMR step montage, plating, the bite in silence, verdict.

- **B-roll:** food macro IS the picture (~80%); talking head only at hook and taste

- **Music:** upbeat from frame 1, cut on every beat, drops out for the bite (silence + crunch), back for the verdict

- **Beats:** hero shot + premise 0-1.5s → ingredients → steps montage → reveal / plating → bite in silence + verdict
