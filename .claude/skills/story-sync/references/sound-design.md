# Sound design

Sound carries emotion more cheaply than picture: it biases what viewers think they saw (Boltz 2001), colours neutral faces (Tan et al. 2007), and loudness alone changes interpretation (Wakefield et al. 2017). Viewers credit sound's contribution to the image (Chion, "added value"); a sound and image at the same instant fuse into one event ("synchresis"). TikTok/Kantar (2021, platform-commissioned): 88% of users say sound is essential to TikTok.

## Layers [RoT; trailer vocabulary]
| Layer | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Room tone / ambience | defines place; covers jump cuts | always under cuts so silence never sounds like a dropout |
| Foley (steps, cloth, product handling) | realism | AI video especially needs it rebuilt in post |
| Whoosh | motion, transitions, whips | starts 4-8 frames before the cut, peaks on it |
| Riser | anticipation | always resolves |
| Impact / boom / braam | weight on reveal or title | premium ≤ 1 per 10-15 s |
| Pop / click / ding | UI, text appearing, "correct" | explainers, listicles |
| Stinger | end of joke/chapter | after the line, not on it |

## ASMR in product content
ASMR raised recall via tingle, immersion and narrative transportation, and audio-ASMR raised attention and purchase intent in an ad study - but reactions split (intimate vs intrusive, misophonia) (Houghton, Wals & Doucé; UF 2023). Use 2-5 s ASMR segments on texture products (powder pour, scoop, shaker, packaging, food). Avoid mouth/eating/wet sounds in health ads.

## Comedy timing with SFX
SFX lands **on** the visual beat (synchresis). Leave 6-12 frames of silence before the punchline SFX. Reaction SFX (boing, crickets, record scratch, vine boom) sparingly: 1-3 per 30 s outside high-energy skits. The funniest SFX is often a *realistic* sound in an absurd context.

## Cold vs hype sound
- **Cold (cruelty, shame, loss):** music drops out; room tone/HF hiss rises; diegetic sounds go dry and close (a cup set down, breathing); optional low drone or one sustained dissonance; **no stingers**. Silence sides the audience with the target (ledger villains rule).
- **Hype (triumph, reveal, launch):** sub-bass hits, crowd swells, risers into drops, wide compressed stereo.

## AI-generated video
Native audio is fine for drafts. For finals rebuild: room tone, foley, licensed/original music, ElevenLabs VO, SFX on cut points (ai-video playbook: hybrid workflows).
