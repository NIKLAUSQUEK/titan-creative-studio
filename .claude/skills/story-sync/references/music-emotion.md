# Music and emotion

## What the research says [R]
- **Two independent dials.** Re-recording one Mozart sonata fast/slow × major/minor: tempo changed arousal but not mood; mode changed mood but not arousal (Husain, Thompson & Schellenberg 2002, *Music Perception* 20). → Set **tempo for energy**, **harmony for valence**.
- **Mixed cues = mixed feelings.** Fast + minor or slow + major make listeners feel happy and sad at once (Hunter, Schellenberg & Schimmack 2008/2010). Use: bittersweet nostalgia (slow + major), defiance (fast + minor).
- **Strongest cues:** mode, tempo, dynamics, articulation, timbre, phrasing; happy = faster, higher, major; sad = slower, lower, minor, soft (Gabrielsson & Lindström 2010 review).
- **Music and voice share an emotional code** (Juslin & Laukka 2003, 145 studies): music "sounds sad" for the same reasons a voice does → match VO prosody to the cue.
- **Body response:** faster tempo raises breathing/heart rate/BP; pauses drop them *below baseline* (Bernardi, Porta & Sleight 2006, *Heart*). Crescendo raises arousal, decrescendo relaxes.
- **Music rewrites the picture:** mood-congruent music shifts how ambiguous scenes are read and remembered (Boltz 2001); colours a neutral face before or after it (Tan, Spackman & Bezdek 2007; not anger); loudness alone changes perceived relationships (Wakefield et al. 2017).
- **Tension** = instability, dissonance, uncertainty about something significant (Lehne & Koelsch 2015).
**Caution:** BPM bands below are starting points [RoT]; research gives direction, not exact bands.

## Mood → music map
| Mood | BPM [RoT] | Harmony / texture | Instruments | Edit behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Curiosity | 90-110 | major/modal, unresolved, sparse | pizzicato, plucks, marimba, light perc | cut on off-beats; J-cuts; gaps for questions |
| Tension | 60-90, or pulse 120+ without melody | dissonance, drones, ostinato | low strings, synth pulse, ticking | shots shorten gradually; no resolution until the turn |
| Suspense | slow / no pulse | sustained, rising | risers, high harmonics, sub-bass | hold long → silence → hit |
| Sadness | 50-75 | minor, legato, soft | solo piano, cello, pads | long takes, L-cuts, reaction holds |
| Warmth / trust | 70-100 | major, consonant, acoustic | acoustic guitar, felt piano, light strings | medium cuts on phrase ends; faces |
| Triumph | 110-140 | major, layering crescendo | drums, brass, choir, big strings | downbeat cuts; build; drop at payoff |
| Humour / quirky | 100-130 | major, staccato | ukulele, bassoon, whistle, pizz | hard music cuts, record-scratch stops, stingers |
| Awe | 60-90 / rubato | wide, suspended chords | pads, choir, reverb strings | wide shots held 4-8 s, slow push-ins |
| Urgency | 130-170 | driving minor/modal | pulsing synth, 16th hats | fast metric montage, countdown text |
| Calm / premium | 60-90 | consonant, minimal, space | felt piano, soft synth | slow dissolves, negative space, low SFX |

## Music arc ↔ story arc [RoT]
| Story beat | Music | Edit |
|---|---|---|
| Hook 0-3 s | signature sound or cold open on a hit - or start silent so the voice owns it | hook visual on the first downbeat |
| Setup | intro/verse, sparse low layers | medium cuts |
| Turn | pre-chorus/riser, or drop to silence | shots start shortening |
| Climax | drop/chorus, full layers | fastest cutting, on downbeats |
| Resolution | breakdown/outro, strip layers | longer holds |
| CTA | button/sting or clean tail under voice | end card ≥ 1.5-2 s |
Trailer model: cold open → act 1 intro → act 2 escalation → act 3 climax → silence → button. Trailer editors cut from stems and add their own hits/risers/whooshes.

## Craft rules
- **On vs against the beat:** viewers prefer aligned audio-visual accents, more so in montage (Lipscomb 1999; Rogers & Gibson 2012). Montage/hype = cut on downbeats (or every 2nd/4th beat); dialogue = cut on speech, music underneath; unease = deliberately off-beat.
- **Mickey-mousing** (music copies action exactly) = animation and skits only; mechanical/condescending in serious work.
- **Comedy hard stop:** music dies (record scratch/tape stop) at the reveal → 0.5-1.5 s silence/room tone → punchline.
- **Silence after a build** = the gut-punch: before a reveal, after a cruel line, under the key claim.
- **Risers always resolve** (hit or silence). Premium: ≤ 1 big impact per 10-15 s. Stinger = end of joke/chapter.
- **Ducking:** 6-12 dB reduction under voice, attack 30-80 ms, release 250-700 ms (0.5-1.5 s on continuous beds to avoid pumping). Studio mix: dialogue -14 LUFS integrated, ≤ -1 dBTP; bed 12-18 dB under voice; SFX 3-6 dB under voice peak; editor default `music.db -19` + sidechain duck.
- **Editing music to length:** cut just before the "1" at a phrase end; phrases are 4/8 bars; loop whole/half phrases; listen for two downbeats landing together after a splice; short crossfades; use stems to drop melody under voice and keep drums.
- **Generated music briefs:** genre + BPM + key/mode + instruments + mood arc + length + where the drop lands. Never "in the style of <artist/song>".
