# Class & status storytelling — hot & rich ↔ broke & struggling (both sides, fairly)

## Fairness rules
- Punch at behaviours, systems and hypocrisy — not at being poor itself, and not at people for things they can't control.
- Every side gets its humanity moment (even the most entitled character has a real fear; even the most broke character has pride, wit and agency).
- No "poverty porn" (suffering staged for views) and no "rich-shaming" that's just envy — show cause, cost and choice.
- Stats about poverty, wealth, mobility: sourced (SOURCES.md), localised per market.
- Debate formats steelman both sides before landing a take.

## Tone dial (pick one per piece, or switch mid-piece for whiplash comedy)
Humour (observational, relatable) · Disdain (sharp satire of entitlement or exploitation) · Wit (dry, clever, understated) · Debate (two sides, real arguments) · Hyperbole (absurd exaggeration — "so broke my bank sends me sympathy cards") · Melodrama (Nollywood/Bollywood-style) · Documentary sincerity.

## Hot & rich — real issues and tropes
Pretty privilege AND its costs (not taken seriously, assumed dumb/shallow, constant attention, safety/stalking, objectification, friendships tested by envy) · "Do they like me or my money?" trust issues · gold-digger suspicion (both directions) · loneliness at the top · inheritance/family-business drama · aging and appearance pressure · surveillance of every outfit and meal · wealth guilt vs flaunting · "poor little rich girl" isolation · security risks · status anxiety (always someone richer) · nepo-baby accusations · burnout behind the highlight reel.
Tropes: the yacht that's somehow sad · the hot person who can't get a serious job interview · the billionaire who doesn't know grocery prices · the trust-fund rebel · the influencer whose whole life is content.

## Broke & struggling — real issues and tropes
Rent vs food vs transport choices · the poverty premium (cheap things cost more over time, overdraft and late fees, pay-day loans) · multiple jobs/gig work, no sick days · healthcare delayed until it's an emergency · shame and hiding it · bills-on-the-fridge anxiety · broken phone/car spiral · "just budget better" advice from people who never had to · generational pressure (sending money home, first-gen guilt) · resourcefulness and hustle culture · community solidarity (aunties, group chats, sharing) · mobility barriers (no network, no safety net) · dignity under pressure.
Tropes: payday king for a day · the "I'll pay you back" friend · instant noodles gourmet · splitting the bill with the rich friend who orders lobster · ingenious fixes (duct tape engineering).

## Squeezed middle (often the most relatable)
Looks fine on paper, one emergency from trouble · lifestyle creep · keeping up with the hot-and-rich friends · housing ladder anxiety.

## Formats
- **Same problem, two bank accounts** (split-screen: a flat tyre, a breakup, a birthday, a hospital bill).
- **Class swap** (day-in-the-life exchange — both learn something).
- **Tier list / ranking debate** ("worst rich problems vs worst broke problems" — comment bait).
- **Hyperbole sketch** (escalating absurd exaggeration on both sides).
- **Decision Ladder** (mature-edge.md) with money at stake — the shortcut, the scam, the gamble, the consequence.
- **Mini-doc** with real interviews (consented) + sourced data.
- **Satire of systems** (landlords, influencer economy, hustle-culture gurus) — fictional archetypes, never real private individuals.

## Localise (localisation.md)
SG: HDB vs GCB/condo, COE and car status, maid culture, tuition arms race, NS · UK: council estate vs Chelsea, cost-of-living crisis · US: student debt, medical bills, gig economy · NG/IN/VN/MY: family obligations, remittances, status displays at weddings.

## Character link
Add `class_background` (old money · new money · comfortable middle · squeezed middle · working poor · first-gen climber) and `money_wound` (the one fear money shapes) to roster cards for richer arcs.

## Villains welcome
A rich villain who mocks someone's wealth to one-up them, a broke character who scams a friend — both are valid antagonists. Build them with references/villains-conflict.md: camera stays with the target, cruelty gets cold sound, consequences land, audiences pick a side.
