## C. Choosing fast
1. Who is speaking (brand file / brain): pick the archetype row.
2. What the story is (genre-playbooks.md §0): pick the genre.
3. Platform + aspect: the preset handles safe zones; add `--aspects`.
4. Learn a new creator: `editor.py study <3-5 links> --name <style>`. It writes a measured preset and a STYLE_STUDY.md; then add a row here.
5. Health brands: apply the compliance pack to every comedy format (no patient testimonials, outcomes or superlatives; skits are clearly fictional).

## D. Engine features these styles use (tools/editor)
- Built and tested:
  - two-level glow captions (`captions.layout: two_level`, key_font, glow);
  - transitions flash, dip and glow (`transitions.default`, `on: beats|cuts`);
  - the font pack (tools/editor/fonts/FONTS.md);
  - B-roll layouts full, pip, split, side and greenscreen;
  - code-motion cards (`broll.kind: motion`: step-cards, stat-counter, compare-loop, kinetic-quote);
  - chats[] texting cutaways;
  - SFX stacks + reveal dips + cut coverage (`sfx.stacks`, `reveal_every_s`, `cut_coverage`);
  - edu-song tool and style study.
- Still to build (specs in research/refs-2026-10-25/editors-A.md and editbymmh-sound.md):

  - framed layout (a rounded window over a pattern);
  - face-avoiding captions;
  - per-scene caption styles and curved text;
  - colour-wash and wand/pixel wipes;
  - type-on ticks and SFX silence beats;
