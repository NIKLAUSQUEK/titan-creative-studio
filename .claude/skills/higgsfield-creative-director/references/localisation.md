# Localisation packs — settings, language, humour, sound, compliance
Gate 1 asks "Which market/locale?" → load the pack(s). A pack changes: settings library, wardrobe, language register, humour sources, edit/sound flavour, casting, and LEGAL rules. Same rules as voices: archetypes, never impersonate a named actor/celebrity; cast and voice from the community; real local VO beats AI accents (AI TTS Singlish/Hinglish sounds fake → record a local speaker or Klaus, then voice_change if needed).

## 🇸🇬 SINGAPORE — Singlish & SG vibes
**Language**: Singlish register — particles lah / leh / lor / meh / sia / hor; code-switching (Hokkien "paiseh", "shiok", "kiasu", "jialat"; Malay "makan", "boleh"; "can or not?", "steady", "on lah"). Calibrate: light Singlish for brand ads (wide SG audience), heavy for skits. Subtitles in standard English when heavy.
**Persona archetypes**: Kopitiam Uncle philosopher · Market Auntie (bargain hunter, "you think I don't know ah?") · NS Encik/buddy energy (keep uniforms generic, no real SAF insignia) · Atas Orchard brunch couple · Kiasu parent · Grab driver life coach · CBD office kaki.
**Settings that render well** (prompt fragments):
- HDB void deck: "Singapore HDB public housing block, void deck with round stone tables and stools, numbered block sign, potted plants, tropical afternoon light"
- Hawker centre/kopitiam: "busy Singapore hawker centre, tray-return area, stall signboards with generic stall names, ceiling fans, plastic chairs, kopi in glass cups"
- MRT carriage: "Singapore MRT train interior, grey bench seats, handrails, commuters, tropical light through windows" (no real line maps/logos)
- BTO flat interior, condo pool deck, East Coast Park cycling path, CBD skyline at dusk, wet market, Jewel-style indoor garden (generic, no trademarks), void-deck badminton, rain-soaked covered walkway.
**Humour sources**: kiasu-ism, queueing culture, aircon wars, "chope" with tissue packets, NS stories, BTO waiting, Grab/food delivery, mum's WhatsApp forwards, weather (hot/raining only).
**Sound flavour**: kopitiam ambience, MRT door chime-style (generic, not the real chime), ah-beng ringtone-style stings, xinyao/acoustic for nostalgia.
**COMPLIANCE (health clients — heal360, Physio Spot SG)**: HCS (Advertisement) Regulations 2021 (Reg 14) + SMC ECEG + professional codes: NO testimonials/endorsements/patient photos reproduced in ads, NO paid reviews, NO superlatives ("best", "leading", "most trusted", "No.1"), NO free consults/discounts as inducements, claims must be substantiable. → Use education, demonstration, process, practitioner explanation; dramatised fictional skits must not pose as real patient testimony. Verify whether the specific clinic is a licensable service under HCSA vs. covered by its professional council's advertising code (e.g. allied health) — check with counsel. General SG: ASAS Advertising Code (legal, decent, honest, truthful).

## 🇮🇳 BOLLYWOOD / Indian
**Style archetypes**:
- **Filmi hero entry**: slow-mo walk, wind-machine hair/dupatta, low-angle, lens flare, crowd parting, bass + dhol hit.
- **TV-serial triple zoom**: shocked face → three snap zooms (1.0 → 1.2 → 1.4) with "dhum-ta-ta" stings — the ultimate reaction stim.
- **Song-and-dance break**: abrupt cut from dialogue into choreographed montage, colour pops, synchronized group.
- **Melodrama confrontation**: rain, thunder flash, mother's tears, "Main tumhe…" dramatic pause.
- **Rain song romance**, **wedding/sangeet chaos**, **filmi dialogue-baazi** (punchy one-liners delivered to camera).
**Language**: Hinglish code-switching; filmi delivery (pause, then punchline). Archetypes only — never impersonate specific stars or reuse famous dialogues verbatim.
**Settings**: haveli courtyard, Mumbai local train door, chai tapri, rooftop terrace with laundry lines, mandap/sangeet stage with marigolds, monsoon street, Indian-family living room with TV serial on.
**Grade**: saturated jewel tones (marigold, magenta, emerald), warm skin, glowy highlights.
**Sound flavour**: dhol, tabla, shehnai stings, sitar swells, harmonium; triple-zoom "dhum" hits.
**Humour sources**: aunty-network gossip, "beta, shaadi kab?", Sharma-ji's son comparison, family WhatsApp, jugaad.

## 🇲🇾 MALAYSIA
Manglish (lah/ma/meh, "can la", "alamak"), mamak stall at night (teh tarik pour), pasar malam, kampung house, KL LRT, Penang shophouses. Humour: mamak debates, durian, balik kampung, traffic. Multicultural casting (Malay/Chinese/Indian). Halal sensitivities for food/drink.

## 🇻🇳 VIETNAM (HCMC)
Motorbike sea at rush hour, alley (hẻm) cafés with tiny stools, Thao Dien villas/cafés, Ben Thanh-style market (generic), rooftop bars, rain + raincoats on bikes. Humour: motorbike chaos, cafe culture, family dinners, haggling. Language: Vietnamese with English subs; Saigon accent.
**COMPLIANCE (VN, verify):** see compliance-packs.md "Vietnam detail".
- Paid ads lead in Vietnamese, with any foreign language below it at no more than 3/4 size (Ad Law Art. 18).
- Label sponsored posts (Law 75/2025).
- Get image consent (Civil Code Art. 32) and blur faces and plates in street and scam content.
- No politics or state topics.
- Generic signage only: no real shop names in scam scenes.

## 🇹🇭 THAILAND

## How to apply
1. Set `locale` in the brief (one primary, optional secondary for bilingual edits).
2. Story directions (Gate 2) should include ≥1 direction that leans fully into the local pack.
3. Asset Bible (Gate 4) uses the pack's setting prompt fragments for @LOCATION plates; avoid real trademarks/signage.
4. Script (Gate 3) marks language per line + subtitle plan.
5. Compliance block in Gate 3 must cite the pack's legal rules.
