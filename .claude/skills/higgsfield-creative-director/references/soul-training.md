# Soul training photo selection (identity quality = input quality)
Target: 20 photos (Higgsfield accepts 5-20; its own guide recommends 20+ for robustness). One person only.
Mix:  8 face close-ups (front, 3/4 L, 3/4 R, near-profile) | 6 chest-up | 4 full-body | 2 wildcards (talking, laughing)
Light: mostly soft daylight, some indoor; no single lighting setup dominating.
Recency: last ~12 months - same hair, beard, weight as the current look.
REJECT: other people in frame, sunglasses/caps over eyes, beauty/app filters, heavy WhatsApp-compressed forwards,
        motion blur, hard shadow over half the face, arm's-length front-camera selfies (>3 of these = wide-angle nose distortion),
        mirror selfies with phone over face, pets/objects covering the face, short edge < 1024 px.
Score each candidate 0-2 on: face visibility, sharpness, lighting, angle diversity contribution, recency. Keep top 20 by score
while enforcing the mix quotas (diversity beats a 21st near-duplicate front shot).
Known bad example: existing Soul "Nik" thumbnail = arm's-length selfie, face cropped at frame edge, dog partially in frame.
