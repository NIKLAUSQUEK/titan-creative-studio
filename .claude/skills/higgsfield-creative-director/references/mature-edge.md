# Mature & Edge module — adult (18+) storytelling, raunch, shock, rage, absurdity

## Hard lines (never, regardless of framing)
No sexually explicit content/porn generation · no bypassing model or platform moderation (no prompt obfuscation, coded wording, or "jailbreak" phrasing) · nothing sexual or suggestive involving minors or characters who could read as minors (students in suggestive arcs = clearly 25+, non-school settings) · no sexualising or deepfaking real people · no real tragedies/real victims as entertainment · no punchlines degrading a race/ethnic/protected group · no fake engagement/click farms · no real-world how-to for crimes (stories show choices and consequences, not instructions) · no health misinformation.

## 1. Morally grey "bad decision" stories (cautionary arcs)
Structure — the **Decision Ladder**: Ordinary want → tempting shortcut (Decision 1) → escalation (Decision 2) → point of no return (Decision 3) → consequence lands → twist/irony → lesson or open question (comment bait: "what would YOU have done?").
- Each decision shown as a fork the audience can see coming ("don't do it…" tension); consequences realistic, specific, proportionate.
- Example (Physio Spot / men's health): man skips protection on a reckless night → ignores symptoms out of shame → asks the group chat instead of a professional → consequence (STI diagnosis, awkward partner conversation) → the clinic scene reframes shame into action → CTA: get tested / see a professional. Dark-comedic or sober versions.
- Series format: "One bad decision a day" — episodic, cliffhanger endings.

## 2. Raunchy & suggestive (non-explicit) — adult brands
For condoms, STI testing, ED/PE (men's health), lingerie-adjacent, adult wellness, PeachForm glute content:
- Innuendo, double entendre, euphemism comedy (food/sports/DIY metaphors), awkward-honest dialogue, "say the quiet part" humour, frank clinical myth-busting.
- Visual: confident, flirty, fitted gym/athleisure, glute-focused training demos, swimwear in context (beach/pool), sweaty-gym energy, suggestive cut-aways (the knock on the door, the fogged mirror, fireworks gag). Fully clothed or context-appropriate swimwear; no nudity; adults clearly 25+; consent and mutual enthusiasm on screen.
- Platform reality: TikTok/Meta restrict sexual-health ads (age targeting 18+, no explicit imagery, product-category rules) → plan an "ad-safe cut" and an "organic edgy cut"; YouTube/X age-gated cuts can go further on language/innuendo, still non-explicit.

## 3. Profanity & aggression dial (set in Gate 1)
0 clean · 1 mild (damn/hell) · 2 strong (sh*t, a**, bleep-able) · 3 uncensored adult. Default = platform-safe for the chosen placement (paid ads ≤1; TikTok organic ≤2, use bleeps/sound-stims as comedy; YouTube age-gated/X up to 3). Aggression = verbal/comedic heat, rants, roasts, screaming matches — no real-world threats, no slurs.

## 4. Shock value & absurdity
- Absurd escalation ("yes-and" logic taken to the extreme), surreal non-sequiturs, Thai-surreal ad energy, Nollywood melodrama, cursed POVs, uncanny mundane horror, freak-accident dark comedy (fictional, no real victims), death/grief stories done with weight or with dark wit.
- Shock is a hook, not the payload: earn it within the story or the audience feels cheated.

## 5. Rage-bait (as a writing technique, not deception)
Hook formats: confident hot take with a real argument behind it · "wrong-on-purpose" setup that the video corrects (e.g. "Stretching is useless" → nuance) · intentional controversy between in-group preferences (pineapple-on-pizza tier, gym-bro debates) · villain-character POV · the obviously flawed choice a character makes that viewers yell at.
Guardrails: no health misinformation left standing, no targeting groups, no fake news about real people; the comment war should be about taste, opinions or choices.

## 6. Japanese-style writing modes
- **Light-novel long title hook**: "I Skipped Leg Day for 7 Years and Now My Physio Says I Walk Like a Crab" — the title is the premise.
- **Isekai / reincarnation framing**: "Reborn as my own lower back".
- **Boke & tsukkomi**: one character absurd (boke), the other the sharp straight-man retort (tsukkomi) — perfect for duo skits.
- **Inner-monologue overdrive**: frozen moment + rapid internal narration (anime-style).
- **Chapter cliffhangers**: "Next time: the MRI results."
- **Tropes**: tsundere, power-of-friendship, training-arc montage, dramatic reveal panels (freeze frame + speed lines).

## 7. "Ugly car crash" retention (story addiction)
Open loops every 3-5 s, dramatic irony (viewer knows, character doesn't), escalating stakes, unreliable narrator, mid-story reversal, episodic series with "Part 2" demand, comment-prompt endings. Pair with reaction faces + stims from voice-persona-audio.md.

## Gate hooks
Gate 1 adds: audience age gate (18+ yes/no), profanity dial 0-3, placement (paid ad / organic / age-gated), edge level (spicy / dark / absurd / rage). Gate 2 directions may include a Decision-Ladder, a raunch, a Japanese-mode and a rage-hook variant. Compliance block lists the platform cut plan (ad-safe vs organic vs age-gated).
