# Sight library: Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon)

Use for any HCMC shoot, try-on preview, skit set or "market the city" post. Each sight lists:
- **Look lock:** what QC checks so a generated or composited image still reads as the real place.
- **Story hook:** a true one-liner for captions and mini-docs. Source it before posting.
- **Light / crowds**, and **rules**.

Facts are marked [verify] where they need a fresh check. Landmark plates are real photos (own shoots, or licensed via `editor.py web-images` with credits kept), never invented. Admin note: HCMC merged with Bình Dương and Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu on 1 Jul 2025 and districts became wards [verify naming]. Say "District 1" in tourist copy only if locals still use it; otherwise use the street name.

## Colonial core (walkable, District 1 / Bến Nghé)
| Sight | Look lock | Story hook | Light / crowds | Rules |

| **Notre-Dame Cathedral** (Nhà thờ Đức Bà) | twin square-topped spires, red brick shipped from France, rose window, Virgin Mary statue in the park in front | "every brick came from Marseille" [verify] | early morning, before tour buses | long restoration since 2017; check for scaffolding before booking [verify]; Snap Saigon spot (park side); modest poses, it's a church |

| **The Café Apartments** (42 Nguyễn Huệ) | old apartment block whose every balcony is a café or shop | "a vertical street of cafés" | evening, when the signs light up | shop signs = brand names: blur or crop in ads |
| **Saigon Opera House** (Nhà hát Thành phố) | white French-Gothic façade, arched entrance, statues | opened 1900; still hosts shows (e.g. the AO Show) [verify current shows] | golden hour, steps facing Lê Lợi | steps used for events: check before booking |

## Street life (the "real Saigon" texture)
- **Hẻm** (alley) cafés with tiny plastic stools, motorbike sea at rush hour, bánh mì carts, rain with ponchos on bikes, phin coffee drip, street barbers.
- **Bùi Viện** (De Tham corner) for nightlife. Snap Saigon spot; Khang's lane.
- **Rules:**
  - Blur faces and plates of bystanders.
  - No real shop names in scam stories.
  - Night-street shots of drinking stay organic, never in paid ads.

## Never / careful
- **Independence Palace:** a VND 500k-per-camera commercial fee from 1 Aug 2026 [source: Tuổi Trẻ, brand file §2]. Leave it off unless priced in.
- **War Remnants Museum and Củ Chi tunnels:** history and remembrance tone only. Never try-on, glam, giveaways or booking CTAs (ledger 2026-10-28).
- **State symbols:** no flags, emblems or political banners as props in brand or paid content. Never imply the state endorses the brand.
- **Drones:** no drone or flycam shots without a permit [verify].
- **Generated landmarks:** they are never presented as a real place. Generic settings get generic names.

## Culture cues to sprinkle (true, light, non-political)
- Cà phê sữa đá, phin filters, the morning ritual on tiny stools.
- Áo dài on Monday office mornings for some workplaces [verify] and on 20/10.
- Nón lá.
- Bánh mì as street breakfast.
- "Đi bão" street celebrations after football wins (Khang's lane).
- Tết: hoa mai (yellow apricot blossom) is the southern Tết flower, while the north uses peach blossom. A strong local-detail hook.
- Mid-Autumn: mooncakes and star lanterns.
- Saigonese warmth and directness; Southern dialect ("dạ", "nha", "hen").

Humour stays on the tourist moment, never on Vietnamese people as a group (brand file §5).
