# Funnels, lead magnets, landing pages, nurture

Use this to design a client funnel end to end. It was researched on 2026-10-25; sources are in `research/sources-agency-playbooks.md` §2. Benchmarks vary widely, so swap in each client's own baseline after 4–8 weeks. Compliance notes here are operational guidance, not legal advice. Where they differ, `compliance-sg.md` and the ledger win.

## 1. Funnel models: which fits which client
| Model | Core idea | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| TOFU / MOFU / BOFU (AIDA) | Content mapped to funnel stage | Shared planning language |
| **See-Think-Do-Care** (Kaushik) | Stages are defined by intent shown in behaviour, not by demographics. Each stage has its own metric: don't judge See content on sales | Media plans and measurement for every client |
| **Demand creation vs capture** (Chris Walker) | Only a few buyers are in-market at any time. Creation happens in "dark social" (DMs, WhatsApp groups, podcasts), which attribution can't see, so add "How did you hear about us?" to every form | B2B (TITAN.AI), long or shared decisions |
| Flywheel | Customers drive growth through referrals, reviews and UGC | E-commerce subscriptions, fitness communities. **Not for SG clinics as a testimonial engine** |

**Fit by client:**
- **Clinics.** See-Think-Do-Care with a local-intent Do stage: Google Search, Google Business Profile (GBP) and Maps, then WhatsApp or booking.
  - Education builds trust *before* the pain episode.
  - Men's health relies on dark social (private searching, shame), so make it anonymous-friendly and route to private channels.
- **Protein e-commerce.** Flywheel plus TOFU-MOFU-BOFU. Lifetime value (LTV) depends on the second order.
- **Women's fitness.** Challenge and community loops. Day-1 activation predicts retention.
- **AI agency.** Demand creation (founder content, teardowns, workshops) plus capture (search, referrals) plus an application funnel.

## 2. Lead magnets
**What converts** (Hormozi *$100M Leads* plus practitioner consensus):
1. **Specific:** one narrow problem for one person.
2. **Quick win:** usable in under 10 minutes, with a result the same day.
3. **Solves part of the core problem** and makes the paid offer the obvious next step.
4. **Better than free alternatives:** personalised beats generic.
5. **Easy to get:** a short form, delivered instantly on the thank-you page plus email or WhatsApp.

**Naming:** [result] + [for whom] + [timeframe or format], e.g. "The 7-Minute Desk Neck Reset (for office workers)". The thank-you page should carry the next step (book, buy or join).

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Checklist / cheat sheet | Fastest to make. Good for top of funnel |
| **Quiz / assessment** | Highest segmentation value (Levesque *Ask*: a deep-dive survey finds the buckets, then the quiz routes people into them). **Clinics:** educational framing ("Which back-pain pattern sounds like you?"), never a diagnosis, plus a "not medical advice" line. Collect minimal health data with explicit PDPA consent |
| Calculator | Protein, macros, ROI |
| Mini-course (3–5 days, email or WhatsApp) | Doubles as nurture |
| Webinar / workshop | Fitness, B2B |
| Template / swipe file / free tool | B2B |
| Challenge (5–30 days) | Fitness |
| Sample / trial | E-commerce sample packs, app trials |
| **Consultation / assessment** | **SG licensable healthcare may NOT advertise a free consult, free assessment, discount or package.** MOH restricts "free", "complimentary", "discount", "promotion", "offer", giveaways and lucky draws. Package prices may only be shown at the point of payment. Use "Book an assessment" at the normal listed price |

**Compliant magnet ideas:**
- **Physio:** Desk Neck & Back Reset (7-min video + PDF) · "Which back-pain pattern is yours?" educational quiz · 4-week Running Injury Prevention plan for marathon season.
- **Men's / pelvic health:** private 5-day "Pelvic Floor 101 for Men" course · IPSS-style symptom questionnaire as "information to bring to your doctor" (nothing stored without opt-in) · "What to expect at your first appointment" explainer.
- **Protein:** protein calculator that recommends a product · 14-day high-protein hawker/pho guide (SG/VN) · break-even sample-pack offer chosen by quiz.
- **Women's fitness:** free 7-day "Strong Start" challenge (WhatsApp Community or app) · "Find your training style" quiz · training planner.
- **AI agency:** AI Opportunity Audit scorecard (15 questions → score + top 3 automations) · prompt/SOP swipe file · monthly 45-min live workshop.

## 3. Funnel types
| Funnel | Flow | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiz** | Ad → quiz (5–10 questions, progress bar, email or WhatsApp gate before the result) → personalised result → tailored offer → segmented nurture | Protein, fitness, AI audit, physio (educational) |
| **Webinar / VSL** (Brunson's "Perfect Webinar": one big belief, 3 secrets that break false beliefs, stack, close) | Register → reminders → live or evergreen → offer → replay deadline | Fitness programmes, B2B |
| **Tripwire / self-liquidating offer** | S$7–27 front end + order bump + upsell. Ads break even on the first purchase | Protein, fitness. **Never clinics** (counts as an inducement) |
| Free + shipping | Free item → upsell | Shaker or sample (e-commerce only) |
| **Application** | Content/VSL → qualifying form (budget, timeline, role) → booked call with routing → proposal | AI agency, high-ticket offers |
| **Challenge** | Register → 5-day challenge → daily lives → day-5 offer with cart close | Women's fitness |
| Product launch (Walker PLF) | Pre-launch → 3 value pieces → open cart → close | Drops, cohorts |
| **E-commerce** | Ad → product page (or advertorial/listicle pre-lander) → cart with order bump → checkout → one-click upsell → subscription prompt → flows | Protein |
| **Local-service booking** | Search/Maps → **GBP** (categories, services, real photos, weekly posts, Q&A, booking link) → service page → WhatsApp click-to-chat (`wa.me/65…?text=` prefilled) or booking widget → reminders | Clinics |
| **Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads** | Meta ad → WhatsApp with prefilled text → greeting + quick-reply buttons → human hand-off → booking or sale | Very strong in SEA |

**WhatsApp Business Platform economics** (Meta docs, read 2026-10-25):
- Per-message pricing since 1 Jul 2025.
- A 24-hour customer-service window opens each time the user messages. Outside it, only pre-approved templates may be sent, and only to users who opted in.
- **From 1 Oct 2026:** service messages beyond 1,000 free per month are charged, as are utility templates sent inside the window.
- CTWA ads opened in the app give a free entry window (commonly 72 h; verify the duration).
- **Rule:** reply to CTWA leads instantly (automated greeting, then a human within minutes) and ask for the booking inside the free window.

## 4. Landing pages and CRO
**Above the fold** (mobile first, ~375 px wide):
1. A headline that matches the ad.
2. A subhead saying who it's for and the outcome or mechanism.
3. A hero image of the real product, practitioner or process (never stock smiles).
4. **One primary CTA.** In SEA, put WhatsApp next to the form or booking button.
5. A trust strip: credentials, registrations, media mentions, payment/security badges, ratings (e-commerce only).
6. Clinics: address, nearest MRT, hours.

**Principles:**
- **Message match.** Ad promise = page headline = form = thank-you page. Build one page per ad angle; don't send paid traffic to the homepage. Remove navigation on paid pages.
- **Form length vs lead quality.** Baymard: the average US checkout has 14.88 fields and can be cut to about 12. Global cart abandonment is about 70%.
  - B2C clinic: name, mobile, preferred time.
  - B2B application: 6–10 qualifying questions is fine; the friction filters.
  - Multi-step forms with easy questions first.
- **Social proof.**
  - E-commerce, fitness and B2B: genuine reviews, UGC, case studies with numbers.
  - **SG clinics:** MOH allows only organic, unpaid, unsolicited reviews, and the clinic must not reproduce them. So no testimonials, star widgets or before/after images on clinic pages. **Use instead:**
    - accurate credentials (AHPC/SMC registration, degree, years of practice)
    - "Your first visit in 4 steps" with real photos
    - educational depth with citations
    - factual media mentions
    - plain fees
    - location, hours and parking
- **Speed.** Core Web Vitals "good" at the 75th percentile: **LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1** (web.dev). Compress hero media and avoid heavy builders and pop-ups.
- **Mobile.** Tap targets ≥44 px; sticky CTA bar (WhatsApp / Book); `tel:` and `wa.me` links.
- **Trust.** Registered name and UEN; PDPA notice at the form; marketing-consent checkbox **not pre-ticked**; returns policy (e-commerce).
- **Benchmarks.** Unbounce 2024: 6.6% median landing-page conversion across industries (the 2020 edition said 3.2%). Use the industry splits.
- **A/B testing.**
  - Before launch, write down the hypothesis, metric, minimum detectable effect (MDE), sample size and stop rule. No peeking; run whole weeks.
  - Sample size per variant: n ≈ 16·p(1−p)/δ². At a 3% baseline and a +20% relative lift, that is about 13,000 visitors per variant. A clinic page with 1k visits a month can't detect small lifts, so:
    - test **big swings** (offer, angle, page type)
    - test creative in-platform (Meta A/B, Google experiments)
    - or run sequential before/after comparisons, labelled as directional
  - Use Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps, replays, rage clicks) plus a one-question poll ("What's stopping you booking today?") for hypotheses.

## 5. Nurture
**Speed-to-lead.** HBR (Oldroyd et al. 2011): firms that contacted a lead within an hour were about 7× more likely to have a meaningful conversation than those that waited another hour, and 60× more likely than those that waited 24 h or more. Treat "5-minute / 400%" claims as unverified.
- **SLA:** instant automated acknowledgement, a human within 5 minutes during opening hours, and an after-hours auto-reply with a booking link.

**Sequence architecture** (email and/or WhatsApp):
1. **Welcome / delivery** (D0): the magnet, expectations, one quick win.
2. **Value** (D1–3): the mechanism, plus a second quick win.
3. **Objection** (D3–6): cost, time, fear, "will it work for me", safety.
4. **Proof.** E-commerce, fitness and B2B: real stories, with consent. **Clinics:** a de-identified "common pattern we see" composite, a process walkthrough, or the evidence base.
5. **Offer** (D5–10). Clinics: a neutral "book an assessment" with no urgency or price inducement. Everyone else: real deadlines only.
6. **Re-engagement** (D30+ or after 60–90 days inactive): "Still want these?" Then sunset non-openers.

**Cadence:** daily for the first 3–5 days, then 1–2 a week. WhatsApp marketing templates 1–4 a month; blocks lower your number's quality rating.

**Segment by:** quiz result or problem · stage (lead, booked, customer, lapsed) · market · language · channel.

**Lead scoring:**
- Fit (0–50): location or service area; role and company size (B2B).
- Intent (0–50): pricing page +10, booking page +15, WhatsApp reply +15, webinar +20, quiz +10.
- Decay of −10 a month.
- Above 50 = MQL. Above 70 = immediate human outreach.

**Pipelines:**
- **Clinic:** New → Contacted → Booked → Attended → Plan accepted → Completed → Re-care.
- **B2B:** Lead → MQL → Discovery booked → held → Proposal → Won/Lost.
- **E-commerce:** Subscriber → 1st order → 2nd order → Subscription → Lapsed.

**Retargeting by stage:**
- **See:** 25–50% video viewers.
- **Think:** non-converting landing-page visitors, quiz starters, 75% viewers.
- **Do:** cart abandoners, booking page without a booking.
- **Care:** upsell, replenishment (about day 25 for a 30-serving tub), and exclusion from acquisition.

**Health retargeting:** Meta bans copy that asserts or implies personal attributes, including health conditions. Write "Pelvic health is a common, treatable issue for men", never "Still struggling with your ED?".

**Awareness-level content** (Schwartz):

| Awareness level | Content |
|---|---|
| Unaware | Pattern-interrupt education |
| Problem-aware | Mechanism explainers, quizzes |
| Solution-aware | Factual comparisons ("physio vs massage vs chiro: what each does"), no denigration |
| Product-aware | Process, credentials, price clarity, FAQs |
| Most aware | Direct booking or buy |

**Consent before any nurture:**
- **SG PDPA + DNC.** Covers voice, SMS and WhatsApp to SG numbers. Check the DNC Registry unless you hold clear consent in evidential form. A message asking for consent is itself a marketing message. Identify the sender and keep timestamped opt-in records.
- **WhatsApp** policy requires opt-in for business-initiated templates.
- **Email/SMS:** Spam Control Act (unsubscribe facility, <ADV> label for unsolicited commercial messages).
- **Other markets:**
  - MY PDPA (2024 amendments).
  - VN PDP Law plus Decree 356 (from 1 Jan 2026; confirm detail with local counsel).
  - UK GDPR/PECR (consent, or soft opt-in for your own customers). Fines raised by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

## 6. Measurement
| Stage | Metrics |
|---|---|
| See | Reach, thumb-stop (3 s view rate), hold rate, CPM, branded search, follows |
| Think | Link CTR, landing-page view rate, LP conversion, CPL, quiz completion, magnet consumption, email clicks (opens are unreliable since Apple MPP) |
| Do | Lead → booked %, show rate, booked → paid %, cost per booking, AOV, checkout conversion, ROAS/MER, CAC |
| Care | Repeat rate, 2nd order within 60 days, churn, plan completion (clinic), reactivation, LTV |

**Unit economics:**
- CAC = all acquisition spend ÷ new customers.
- LTV = AOV × purchase frequency × margin × lifespan.
- Aim for LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1.
- **Payback:** e-commerce on the first order or within 60–90 days; clinics on full plan value; B2B can take 3–12 months.
- **MER** (revenue ÷ total marketing spend) is the sanity check against platform ROAS.

**Attribution:**
- **iOS ATT.** Use Meta CAPI plus Google Enhanced Conversions, deduplicated by event_id.
- **Offline clinic conversions.** Meta retired the Offline Conversions API (May 2025), so send booked, attended and paid events via CAPI with `action_source=physical_store/system_generated`. **Send event names only, never conditions or service names.**
- UTMs on every link, stored in hidden form fields and passed to the CRM.
- CTWA leads: capture the ad ID from the referral data your BSP passes through.
- A self-reported attribution field on every form.
- Geo or holdout lift tests once spend allows (marketing-effectiveness.md).

**Weekly one-page report:**
1. Spend, leads, CPL, bookings, CAC, MER vs target, against the 4-week average.
2. Stage conversion, with the biggest leak flagged.
3. Top 3 and bottom 3 creatives (hook rate, CTR, CPA).
4. Speed-to-lead median, % answered within 5 min, show rate.
5. Self-reported attribution mix.
6. Tests running.
7. Compliance flags and ad rejections.
8. Next week's 3 actions.

Then `/review` → `/learn`.

## 7. Blueprints (fill per client; save to `marketing/strategy/<brand>-funnel.md`)
### A. SG physio clinic (Physio Spot / heal360 physio)
- **Stages:**
  - See: TikTok/Reels/Shorts education.
  - Think: Desk Reset magnet + back-pain pattern quiz + retargeting.
  - Do: Google Search ("[condition] physio [area]") + GBP + CTWA + booking.
  - Care: home-exercise library, recall.
- **Ad angles** (education and process only):
  - "3 desk habits that load your neck"
  - "What actually happens at a physio assessment"
  - "Physio near Tampines MRT, evenings & Saturdays"
  - Running season: "Knee pain on long runs? Here's what's usually going on"
- **Landing page:**
  1. Headline matching the ad
  2. Conditions
  3. First visit in 4 steps, with real photos
  4. Team credentials
  5. Approach
  6. Plain fees
  7. Location, MRT, hours
  8. FAQ
  9. "Book an assessment" / "WhatsApp us about appointments"
  10. PDPA notice
  - No reviews widget, "free" or "best".
- **Nurture:**
  - D0 Desk Reset
  - D1 "Why your neck tightens by 3 pm"
  - D3 "When self-care is enough, and when it isn't" (red flags)
  - D5 "What happens at an assessment"
  - D7 "A common pattern we see in desk workers" (composite)
  - D10 neutral booking CTA
  - D30 new micro-stretches
  - Post-visit: D1, D14, D42.
- **KPIs:** CPL, lead→booked (25–40% as an internal starting target), show rate ≥85% with reminders, assessment→plan accepted, sessions per patient, cost per first visit, GBP actions, speed-to-lead.

### B. Men's / pelvic-health clinic (sensitive)
- **Stages:** clinical-wording education → private mini-course or self-check → discreet WhatsApp/booking → confidential follow-up.
- **Ad angles** (third person, no implied personal attributes):
  - "Pelvic floor issues are common in men and often treatable"
  - "What a men's health consult involves"
  - "Urinary changes after 40: what's normal"
  - "Private WhatsApp enquiries"
- **Landing page:**
  1. Calm, clinical hero
  2. Conditions in clinical language
  3. Privacy section (discreet billing only if true, private rooms, data handling)
  4. Accurate credentials (no "specialist" unless accredited)
  5. Process
  6. Citations
  7. FAQ ("Is it embarrassing?")
  8. Private WhatsApp / Book
- **Discreet subject lines:** no condition names in previews.
- **Pixels:** no condition data sent to ad pixels.
- **Compliance:** HCSA ad regs + SMC + AHPC; Meta personal-attribute and adult policies; Google healthcare policy; age 25+ (studio rule).

### C. Protein e-commerce (Power Protein; SG/MY/VN)
- **Stages:** creator/UGC/recipes → calculator quiz → product page or bundle → checkout + upsell → replenishment, subscription, referral.
- **Product page:**
  1. Benefit headline + flavours
  2. Price per serving
  3. Subscribe-and-save
  4. Nutrition panel + lab test
  5. Recipes
  6. Genuine reviews/UGC
  7. Comparison table
  8. Shipping and returns
  9. FAQ
  10. Sticky add-to-cart
- **Flows:**
  - Welcome: D0 code → D1 hawker meals → D3 "why isolate" → D5 UGC → D7 code expires (only if true).
  - Abandoned checkout: 1 h / 24 h / 48 h.
  - Post-purchase: D1 / D10 / D22 reorder / D35 subscribe / D60 winback.
- **Compliance:**
  - SFA and HSA: no disease claims.
  - CCS: no fake reviews, urgency or drip pricing.
  - VN: supplement ad-content confirmation + "not a medicine" disclaimer + Law 75/2025 influencer duties.
  - MY: Food Act claims rules.

### D. Women's fitness programme or app (PeachForm; SG/UK)
- **Stages:** strength-positive, transformation-free content → "Find your training style" quiz + 7-day challenge → trial or cohort → community, streaks, referral.
- **Challenge flow:**
  - D−1 kit list
  - D1–5 daily workouts + WhatsApp nudge
  - D3 soreness tips
  - D5 live session
  - D6 doors open
  - D8 FAQ
  - D10 close (only if true)
- **Compliance:** Meta weight-loss/body-image policy (no before/after, 18+ for weight-loss offers); UK ASA/CAP + DMCC subscription rules; real deadlines only.

### E. AI agency B2B (TITAN.AI; SG/MY/UK)
- **Stages:**
  - Creation: founder LinkedIn, teardowns, podcasts, SME associations.
  - Capture: vertical search keywords, referrals.
  - Convert: AI Opportunity Audit → application → discovery → proposal.
  - Care: QBRs, expansion, referral.
- **Landing page:**
  1. Outcome headline
  2. Who it's for (10–200 staff)
  3. Problem
  4. Method (Audit → Build → Train → Support)
  5. Case studies with numbers (written consent)
  6. Founder credibility
  7. "Engagements from S$X"
  8. FAQ (PDPA, data, ownership)
  9. Application form
- **Nurture:**
  - D0 score + top 3 automations
  - D2 workflow map
  - D4 case study
  - D7 buying mistakes
  - D10 PDPA explained
  - D14 apply
  - D21 workshop
  - Score ≥70 → founder outreach within 1 hour.
- **KPIs:** audit completions, MQL→SQL, discovery show rate, win rate, cycle days, ACV, CAC payback, pipeline coverage 3×, share of self-reported "dark social" attribution.
