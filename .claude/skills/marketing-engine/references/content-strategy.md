## 4. Funnel + format mix
| Stage | Job | Formats | KPI (analytics-loop.md) | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | stop the scroll, earn a follow | skits, myth-busts, trend-adapted shorts, founder hot takes | reach, 3 s hold, shares, follows | follow / watch part 2 |
| Consideration | "I understand + I trust him" | YouTube explainers, carousels, podcast clips, LinkedIn essays, "what happens at a session" | retention, saves, comments, profile visits | save / read / subscribe to newsletter |
| Conversion | safe, easy next step | process walkthrough, clinic tour, FAQ, booking how-to, case *scenario* (generic, never testimonial) | link clicks, WhatsApp starts, bookings | book / WhatsApp (no free-consult or discount inducement for SG health) |
| Retention | keep patients/customers + referrals by word of mouth (organic, not incentivised) | newsletter, home-programme reminders, community Q&A, BTS | opens/clicks, replies, repeat bookings | reply / share with someone |
Rule of thumb starting mix (adjust by review data): 60% awareness, 25% consideration, 10% conversion, 5% retention.

## 5. Cadence: Hero → Hub → Help
From Google/YouTube's "Hero, Hub, Hygiene/Help" content framework (YouTube Creator Playbook, 2014).
- **Hero** (1/month or quarter): big piece - mini-doc, founder film, flagship ad, long podcast episode. Feeds the waterfall (repurpose-waterfall.md).
- **Hub** (weekly): recurring series people come back for (§7). Same day, same format.
- **Help** (always-on): search-intent answers - "why does my pelvis hurt when I sit", YouTube/blog SEO via vidIQ `vidiq_keyword_research`.

**Aggressive - daily+ (needs studio + editor)**

- Only go aggressive when the review shows the bottleneck is volume, not hook quality.

## 8. Founder-led vs brand-led
| | Founder-led (Klaus's accounts) | Brand-led (brand accounts) |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | first person, stories, opinions | "we", educational, calm |
| Best for | trust, reach, hiring, B2B | conversion, search, local discovery |
| Formats | talking head, story, essays, LinkedIn | skits, explainers, process, FAQ, ads |
| Compliance | SG health rules still apply to any clinic promotion | full SG health rules |
Rule of thumb split: 60/40 founder/brand while audiences are small; founder posts point to brand assets, not the reverse.
