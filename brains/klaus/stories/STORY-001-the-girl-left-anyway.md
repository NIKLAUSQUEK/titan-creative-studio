# STORY-001: The girl left anyway
- **Truth status:** fiction (parable written from a principle; never present as Klaus's or a client's real story)
- **Era / theme:** Young adult / love, body, self-worth, consistency
- **Source:** Klaus's brief, 2026-10-21

## What happened
- A guy gets dumped. He turns the pain into gym sessions: "revenge body".
- Months later he's in the best shape of his life. He sees her again, or she messages. She doesn't come back (or she does and he realises he doesn't want her).
- Sitting in the car after, he realises what he actually built: discipline, a routine, confidence, friends at the gym, a body that works.
- That person, not the body, is who the right partner eventually meets.

## Emotional arc
rejected → furious/driven → proud → gutted (she left anyway) → quiet clarity → hope

## Lesson and wisdom note
- Lesson: the reward of the work is who the work turns you into, not the person you did it for.
- Wisdom note: "You didn't lose her. You lost the reason. The gains stayed."

## Trope
- "Revenge body / glow-up wins them back": **challenge then subvert.** Agree the glow-up is real; reject that it's for them; flip the prize to the self you built, which then attracts someone better suited.

## Values it proves
- Consistency over motivation; self-respect; becoming > getting.

## Hooks
1. "I got the six-pack. She still left. Best thing that ever happened to me."
2. "Revenge bodies don't work. Here's what does." (contrarian)
3. "Nobody tells you what happens AFTER the glow-up." (curiosity gap)
4. Cold open: car park, gym bag on the seat, phone lights up with her name. He doesn't open it.
5. "Your ex is the worst reason to go to the gym, and the best reason to start."
