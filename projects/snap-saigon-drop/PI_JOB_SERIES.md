# "The PI Job": two drama series (Klaus, 29 Oct 2026)

Both series use the same engine as EP 1. Each comes as a reel series (3-4 parts) plus a story carousel.

Honesty line on every part (end card, small): *"snap saigon shoots photos, not PI work 😅 (this one's a story)"*. It keeps the hook while making clear we don't sell surveillance, and the AI-animated cast makes the fiction obvious. Never name a real business in the cheating storyline.

---

## Series A: "My customer's boyfriend cheated on her… with me" (snapper POV, Westerner)
**Cast:**
- **Hân** (our snapper, the narrator)
- **Trâm** (the customer, 26, Saigon office girl)
- **Jake** (Trâm's boyfriend of 3 months, 32, American, a loud eagle-and-flag tee, forward and handsy, always on his phone)

**Hook variations:**
- "my customer's boyfriend cheated on her… with me"
- "the weirdest booking I've ever taken"
- "she hired me to follow her boyfriend. I found him on my Tinder"

**Part 1, "The Booking":**
1. A WhatsApp from Trâm: "hi, is this snap saigon? weird request…"
2. "my bf says he has a 'late client meeting' tonight. won't say where. he's ALWAYS on his phone."
3. "I know where he works. can you follow his day and take photos? 👀"
4. Hân, deadpan into the camera: "I'm a wedding photographer."
5. Trâm: "I'll pay double."
6. Hân: "…send the address."

Ends with "Part 2 →".

**Part 2, "The Tail":**
1. Hân with her digicam, hiding behind a bánh mì cart, a sugarcane stall, a motorbike seat.
2. Jake leaves the office, checks his phone, smiles at it, and gets in a Grab towards D1.
3. Hân's shift ends. She texts Trâm: "my booking's done, I have a dinner date tonight 😅"
4. Trâm: "PLEASE. I'll book 2 more hours on the website now 🙏". A booking-confirmed notification pops up.
5. Hân sighs: "fine."

Ends with "Part 3 →".

**Part 3, "The Date":**
1. Hân, still tailing Jake near Bitexco, opens Tinder to message tonight's date, "J".
2. Hân: "where are we meeting? I'm near Bitexco"
3. J: "omg me too 😂 I'm at the café right at the bottom of the tower"
4. Hân looks up from her phone. Across the street, at the café, Jake is texting and smiling.
5. Her phone buzzes: "J: just got here, I'm in the corner 😉"
6. A slow push-in on Hân's face, music cuts out, a phone-chime sting.
7. On-screen text: **"my customer's boyfriend… was my date."**

Ends with "Part 4: what I did next 👀".

**Part 4, "The Shoot" (payoff):**
1. Hân sits down opposite Jake, smiling. "Hi J. Smile for the camera." Click, click, click.
2. Cut: Trâm opens the delivered gallery.
3. Final scene: Trâm and Hân on a rooftop doing a glow-up "revenge shoot" together, laughing, best friends now.
4. On-screen line: *"She came for proof. She left with the best photos of her life."*

**Carousel version** (8 slides, chat screenshots + key frames):
1. Hook: "my customer's boyfriend cheated on her… with me"
2-3. Trâm's booking chat
4. The tail (key frame)
5. "please, I'll pay extra" + booking-confirmed notification
6. The Tinder chat: "omg me too"
7. Key frame: Jake in the café window
8. "comment PART 4 and I'll tell you what I did next 👀"

## Series B: "The PI Job, Việt Kiều edition" (situational comedy, it's all a misunderstanding)
**Cast:**
- **Linh** (the customer, 24, Saigon, dating Kevin for 6 weeks)
- **Kevin** (a Việt Kiều from California, 27, very sweet, very busy this week)
- Kevin's family, just arrived from the US: two sisters, three cousins, and a loud aunt

**Part 1, "Suspicious":**
1. Linh to the snapper: "he cancelled 3 dates this week. my friend saw him with a girl in D1. AND another girl in D3. AND ANOTHER at the airport 😤"
2. Hân: "…how many girls?"
3. Linh: "FIVE."

**Part 2, "The Evidence":** a montage of increasingly damning photos.
1. Kevin hugging a girl at arrivals.
2. Kevin holding a girl's handbag.
3. Kevin feeding a girl phở.
4. Kevin in a jewellery shop with two girls.

Linh's texts get angrier: "🔪", "I KNEW IT".

**Part 3, "The Reveal":**
1. Linh storms into the restaurant.
2. Twelve people turn around: the whole family, from his aunt to the cousins, on a family trip.
3. The aunt: "Ơ, con là Linh hả?! Kevin talks about you EVERY DAY!"
4. Kevin, sweating, holding a ring box from the jewellery shop.

**Part 4 (button):** the snapper ends up shooting the whole-family photo, with Linh in the middle.

On-screen line: *"she hired a PI. she got a family photo."*

**Carousel version:** "the 5 girls my customer thought her bf was cheating with", one photo per slide, then the reveal "they're his sisters, cousins and aunt 😭".
