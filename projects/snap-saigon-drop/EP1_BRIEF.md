## Notes from @vi_skh's animated series (vidIQ, 29 Oct 2026; Bugville Part 7, 2.6M plays)
- **Look:** polished Pixar/DreamWorks-style 3D. Warm golden window light by day, deep navy moonlight against warm streetlamps by night. Large expressive eyes, exaggerated reactions.
- **Shots:** 1-3.5 s each. Slow push-ins, reaction close-ups, tracking shots for action. No on-screen captions in the animated series; the drama episodes use subtitles.
- **Sound:** an orchestral or acoustic score. Big SFX on every beat (whoosh, thud, gasp).
- **Endings:** each episode ends unresolved ("Part 8" next). Our version: every EP ends on the drop and "follow for EP 2"; the next EP picks up the couple's story in Saigon.
