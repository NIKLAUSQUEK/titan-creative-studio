# Snap Saigon "Photos of Us" series: EP 2-6 scripts (draft, Klaus to pick)

Klaus on EP 1 (29 Oct 2026): "kinda on point!! more scripts and variation and humour like this would be good."

**Same engine every time:**
1. **Cold open:** an animated cliffhanger of static 1-2 s close-ups with short subtitles, building to a peak.
2. **Hard cut to the outburst:** "I'm going to Ho Chi Minh, b*tch!" The line is bleeped on the brand account and only added in the edit.
3. **The drop:** BTS and finished photos, crash zooms, "don't stress".
4. **End card:** "follow for EP N+1".

Each episode uses a different kind of humour so we can test which lands. Cast: Mia and Jay (the couple), Ben (the unsure tourist), Hân and Khang (our Gen Z guides).

| EP | Title | Humour style | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Mum Says No | polite British panic | Ben |
| 3 | The Missed Flight | action-thriller parody | Hân + Khang |
| 4 | The Group Chat | texting suspense | Mia + Jay (continues EP 1) |
| 5 | The Boss | deadpan office | Ben |
| 6 | Ghost Town | meta anti-joke (Tết) | Khang |

---

## EP 2 "Mum Says No" (polite panic)
Title card: *Mum Says No*, with "every traveller has heard 'are you SURE that's safe?'"
1. **CU Ben on a video call in his bedroom**, map on the wall. Mum (offscreen voice, phone screen glow): "Vietnam? On your OWN?"
2. **CU phone screen:** Mum's face fills it, reading glasses on. "The traffic. The scams. You'll get lost."
3. **CU Ben**, a polite nod, sweating: "Mm. Yes. Fair."
4. **Insert:** Ben's thumb hovering over a "Book flight" button.
5. **CU Mum:** "Promise me you won't go."
6. Black, silence.
7. **HARD CUT:** Ben at the Tân Sơn Nhất arrivals hall, backpack on, arms wide, yellow light: "Mum. I'm going to Ho Chi Minh, b*tch." (he's already there)
- **Drop:** Hân meets him, the street-crossing BTS, his finished photos.
- **Caption:** "send this to the friend whose mum still says no 👀"

## EP 3 "The Missed Flight" (thriller parody)
Title card: *Final Call*, with "the 6 a.m. flight nobody wakes up for".
1. **ECU alarm clock:** 6:47. A ticking sound.
2. **Hân sprints through the airport** (tracking side shot), hair flying, digicam bouncing.
3. **Departure board** flips: "SGN · FINAL CALL".
4. **CU gate agent's hand** on the door, closing slowly.
5. **Hân slides on her knees** to the gate… the door clicks shut. Her face drops.
6. Black, silence.
7. **HARD CUT:** Khang at the next counter, cap backwards, waving two tickets, deadpan: "Relax. We're going to Ho Chi Minh, b*tch!" (she was trying to LEAVE Saigon; he booked a staycation shoot instead)
- **Drop:** the two of them shooting around Saigon, the photos.
- **Caption:** "tag the friend who's always late ⏰"

## EP 4 "The Group Chat" (texting suspense; continues EP 1)
Title card: *Read 9:41*, with "the trip that almost didn't happen".
1. **Texting cutaway** (editor `chats[]`): group chat "SAIGON TRIP 🇻🇳". Jay: "so who's actually coming?" Typing dots. "can't, work" / "next year!!" / "😭 sorry".
2. **CU Jay on the hotel bed** from EP 1, alone, the camera in his lap.
3. **The typing dots return:** Mia is typing… Mia is typing…
4. **CU Jay's face**, bracing.
5. **The message lands:** "booked us a snapper. 7am. Pink Church. don't be late 📸"
6. **CU Jay:** slow realisation, eyes watering, grin.
7. **HARD CUT:** Jay bursts out onto the balcony at sunrise, arms up: "We're going to Ho Chi Minh, b*tch!"
- **Drop:** Mia and Jay's first photo together. The payoff to EP 1: she was on her phone planning this.
- **Caption:** "she was planning it the whole time 🥹 · EP 5 tomorrow"

## EP 5 "The Boss" (deadpan office)
Title card: *Annual Leave*, with "some requests take more courage than others".
1. **Wide shot:** a grey office, Ben at his desk, fluorescent hum.
2. **CU the boss's shadow** falls over the desk: "We need you this weekend. And next. And…"
3. **CU Ben's coffee cup**, trembling.
4. **CU Ben**: a long blank stare, one eye twitching.
5. **Insert:** an email draft, "Re: annual leave". The cursor blinks.
6. Black, silence.
7. **HARD CUT:** Ben slams the laptop shut, stands up on the chair, tie off: "I'm going to Ho Chi Minh, b*tch!" Out-of-office auto-reply pops up on screen: "Out until I have good photos."
- **Drop:** Ben in Saigon, the photos.
- **Caption:** "this is your sign to finally use your annual leave 🏝️"

## EP 6 "Ghost Town" (meta anti-joke, for Tết)
Title card: *Tết*, with "the one week Saigon goes quiet".
1. **Wide shot:** an empty Saigon street, every shutter down, a leaf blowing past, eerie wind.
2. **Khang stands alone** in the middle of the road, cap backwards.
3. **CU his phone:** a group chat, everyone "home for Tết 🧧".
4. **CU Khang:** lip trembles, dramatic violin.
5. **He takes a deep breath**, fists clenched, about to yell…
6. **HARD CUT:** Khang: "I'm going to Ho Chi Minh, b*t…" He stops and looks around. "…wait. I'm already here." Then a deadpan look to camera.
- **Drop:** Hân arrives on her scooter. Empty-street photos at Tết are the best photos of the year (true: the city empties around Tết).
- **Caption:** "Saigon at Tết = empty streets = the best photos of your life 📸 · DM TET"

---

**Format notes:**
- EP 1 + EP 4 make a two-part arc (the setup and the reveal). Post them on consecutive days.
- For A/B testing, every episode can swap its outburst speaker (Ben, Hân, Khang) as a variation.
- Production per episode is about 100 credits at the draft stage: 7 stills (~10.5) + 7 × 480p clips (~84).
