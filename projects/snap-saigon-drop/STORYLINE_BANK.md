# Snap Saigon storyline bank: 22 drama/comedy stories (vidIQ research, 29 Oct 2026)

**Research:**
- vidIQ account discovery for "AI-animated relationship drama" accounts.
- Two outlier searches: AI drama (cheating, revenge, twists) and travel couple / scam / misunderstanding storytimes. Last 3-4 months, English, ≥10K views, ranked by outlier score (views ÷ creator median).

**What the winners share:**
1. **The line is the hook.** They open on dialogue, not scenery: "Please let me go to the party!", "Why is your ex my bridesmaid", "mom why is dad kissing that lady".
2. **Misread → twist.** The audience is shown evidence of cheating, then it flips: a sister, a rehearsal, a surprise.
3. **The underdog glows up**, then gets cold revenge (indifference beats a tantrum).
4. **Series format:** "Part 1-9", "EP 42", ending unresolved.
5. **Travel versions win on expectation vs reality and on being the victim of something** (scams, language barrier, flirting gone wrong).

**Brand fit:**
- Every story pays off with something we actually sell: 1-hour shoots, 40 photos + 3 clips the same day, Junior/Senior snappers, up to 6 people, custom itineraries over WhatsApp, a rain plan, 6 meeting points.
- Snappers never do anything shady. The fiction line goes on drama posts.
