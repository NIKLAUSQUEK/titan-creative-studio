## Rules
1. A shot not generated from its plate is rejected.
2. Same lens per camera position. Do not "vary" the angle for variety.
3. One visual language: no flat mock-ups cut between 3D shots. Phone content appears as a screen inside the shot or as a call overlay on the character's own footage.
4. The call is the scene: Trâm and Hân talk across it with eyeline-matched shot/reverse-shot (Hân looks screen-right toward her phone; Trâm looks at the lens).
5. Self-QC before any video credit: layout, outfit, light direction, lens, hand/phone continuity.
