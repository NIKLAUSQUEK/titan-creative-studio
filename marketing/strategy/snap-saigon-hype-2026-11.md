## N7. "Lost your passport in Saigon? Do this" · week 4 (AI characters: no)
- **Tone:** serious and helpful (ledger [listicles]). Carousel of numbered steps over calm footage, with sources on the last slide.
- **Steps (sources: Australian Consulate HCMC, vietnamspot; verify for 2026):**
  1. **Police report first:** in the district where you lost it. [Reported: within 2 working days; not reporting can be fined VND 0.5-2M under Decree 144/2021, verify.]
  2. **Your embassy or consulate** issues a new or emergency passport. Bring the police report, photos and an ID copy.
  3. **New visa / exit visa** from the Immigration Department (HCMC office: 333-337 Nguyễn Trãi, D1 [verify]). Takes about 3-7 working days, fee ~$25 [verify]; may need your sponsor.
  4. **Then you can fly.** Rebook flights once the visa date is known.
- **Cons to know:**
  - a whole-day process;
  - few English signs;
  - bring a Vietnamese-speaking friend or translator;
  - photocopy everything, and keep a photo of your passport and visa on your phone from day 1.
- **Last slide:** "Save this. Hope you never need it." No jokes.
