## 1. Positioning (one sentence) + proof points

**For** men in Singapore aged 25-55 who live with erectile, ejaculatory, pelvic or prostate-related problems and say nothing because of shame, **Physio Spot is** a men's health physiotherapy clinic **that** opens the conversation with deadpan humour and then gives a calm, physio-led assessment and a conservative-care plan, **because** it is run by a men's health physiotherapist [confirmed] and pelvic floor physiotherapy has published evidence for some of these conditions (sources below). `[draft]` wording

| Proof point | Status | Source / substantiation |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic scope: ED, PE, pelvic pain, prostate conditions | [confirmed] | `brands/physio-spot.md`; v4 brief §1 |
| Physiotherapist-led care | [confirmed] role. **CONFIRM** exact registration wording (e.g. "registered physiotherapist, AHPC") before it goes on screen | compliance-sg.md §2: credentials stated accurately, "specialist" only if formally recognised |
| Pelvic floor exercises can help some men with ED | evidence-supported, phrase as "can help / for some men" | Dorey G et al. *BJU Int* 2005;96(4):595-7 (RCT, n=55), PubMed 16104916 - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16104916/ |
| Pelvic floor rehabilitation has been studied for lifelong PE | emerging evidence (small, uncontrolled), phrase as "being studied / early evidence" | Pastore AL et al. *Ther Adv Urol* 2014;6(3):83-8 (n=40, uncontrolled), PubMed 24883105 - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24883105/ |
| Myofascial physical therapy is used for chronic pelvic pain syndromes, incl. in men | feasibility evidence only, phrase as "a recognised option to assess for" | FitzGerald MP et al. *J Urol* 2009;182(2):570-80 (feasibility RCT, n=48, 23 men); trial NCT00434343 - https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00434343 |
| ED is common in Singapore | usable with the year stated | Tan et al. 2003: 51.3% of 729 men aged 30+ in a Singapore population sample reported some degree of ED (IIEF-5) - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12762559/ . The data is 20+ years old, so say "a Singapore study found about half of men 30+ reported some degree of ED" and give the year |

Never claim: best / leading / No.1 / most trusted / "only clinic", outcome percentages framed as our results, "cure", "permanent", "in X sessions". Do not quote study success rates (e.g. Dorey's 40% at 6 months) in clinic promotion. They read as an outcome promise. Mechanism and "evidence suggests" only.

## 7. Compliance notes (the items that bite this brand)

- **No testimonials, reviews, star ratings, before/after, patient photos/video, "X men helped".** No patient stories as proof, even de-identified.
- **No superlatives or comparatives.** No "best/leading/No.1/top/most trusted", no "better than pills/doctors".
- **No inducements.** No free consult, discount, promo, "limited slots". **Do not tie posts to 11.11 (11 Nov) or Black Friday (27 Nov) sales.** Not even as ideas.
- **No guarantees.** No cure, permanent, 100%, "in 3 sessions".
- **Sourced claims only.** Each claim goes in the project's `SOURCES.md`. Phrase as "can help / for some men / evidence suggests".
- **Skits are dramatisation.** Actors are never patients. Add "Dramatisation" to caption/on-screen where useful, and the education lands on the end card (ledger heal360/Physio Spot rules).
- **Ads (paid):** third-person wording only (Meta personal-attributes policy: never "Do you have ED?"), function framing (not "last longer"), no suggestive visuals, crude hooks only in organic posts and only in post. Target 25+.
- **Movember:** this is a Movember Foundation campaign name. Use it only as a descriptive reference to the month. No logo, no implied partnership, no fundraising claims `[draft]`. Use "men's health month" in ads.
- **Credentials:** "registered physiotherapist" only once CONFIRMED, with the exact wording. No "specialist" unless formally recognised.
- **Lead capture / newsletter:** PDPA consent, purpose, unsubscribe. Check DNC before SMS/calls.
- Not legal advice. Confirm with counsel which code governs the clinic (HCSA licensable vs AHPC code).
