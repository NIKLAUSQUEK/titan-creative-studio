# Snap Saigon: 25-lever iteration plans for the two likeliest winners (mock)
Source map: ad-strategy.md §10b, built from @gurnoorsinghofficial's "25 iterations on a winning ad" reel; notes are in research/refs-2026-10-25/ads-iterations.md.
**Rule:** start from a proven winner and change ONE lever per variant. Everything else stays byte-identical: the same cut, VN/EN text, end card, music, ad copy and audience. Name each variant `<winner>_it<rung>-<lever>[-x]`. Move down the order only when the cheaper levers stop finding lift.
**Status:** pre-launch mock. Neither ad has won anything yet. These plans run only after `/review` shows a provisional winner (round-1.md §5: CPA ≤ target with ≥5 bookings, or an outlier hook rate + CTR at the L+7 read).
**Compliance on every variant:** the [PAID] and [CMP] blocks from mock-drafts.md still apply. Any new VN line needs a native check. Any new claim must sit in matrix.md "proof we can legally use" first.

## Why these two
- **C01 "The Designated Photographer" (skit, A1).** The pain needs no explaining, the format looks like friend-group Reels rather than an ad, and it has the most levers that are free to pull (text, hooks, cuts). Its risk: comedy can win attention and lose clicks.
- **C04 "My selfie vs 60 seconds with a snapper" (process, A5).** This is the only draft that *shows the product working*. It is the legal substitute for the testimonials we can't use, and it answers the "cringe?" objection directly. Its risk: a lower hook rate than the skit.

Together they test the two halves of the job: pain (C01) and proof (C04). That way a single result tells us which half the market lacks.

## C. Diagnosis shortcuts (read §9 first, then pull these levers)
Metric definitions follow round-1.md (hook rate = 3-s plays ÷ impressions; hold = ThruPlays ÷ 3-s plays). All baselines are TBD, and we compare ads within R1, not against outside benchmarks.

| Symptom (vs R1 peers) | Broken stage | C01: pull first | C04: pull first | Then |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low hook rate | the first frame/line doesn't name the pain, or looks like an ad | 11 (H02, H04), 17 (gallery-first), 22 (persistent header) | 11 (H13, H12), 17 (Landmark 81 backlight), 22 (header label) | 14 curiosity gap |
| Hook OK, hold drops before the reveal/edit | slow body, no escalation | 20 (fast), 14 (count hidden), 12 (mockumentary) | 20 (fast), 14 (reverse order), 21 (12 s) | 19 style swap |
| Hold OK, CTR low | no clear next step / offer unclear | 25 (spoken CTA), 8 (promise b "same day"), 10 (Half-Day) | 25 (a), 8 (b), 24 (per-photo) | the price line earlier in the edit, over the footage (rung 2) |
| CTR high, few bookings | landing mismatch, price or terms shock at checkout, wrong placement | 10, 24, plus deep-link to the Postcard page; check whether the no-refund line surprises people (put it in the ad earlier, not later) | same + a congruent page showing the 4 moves above the booking button | an ad-specific landing page (funnel diversity [LM]) |
| Bookings but CPA rising week on week | fatigue | the next unused hook, 16 creator, 15 format (C05 texting) | 16 creator, 17 opening, 15 carousel | new matrix row (rung 6) |
| Comments ask "is this legit?" | trust gap, not creative | 23 authority (the snapper on camera), run C02/C07 organically | 23 (b, the editor), 18 (c, the edit screen recording) | fix the site trust signals (round-1.md blocker 1) |
| Comments say "cringe" / "staged" | the skit reads as fake | 9 (warmth), 15 (vox-pop) | the honesty label bigger, 18 (camera-back view) | move spend to the other draft |

**One variable per variant, always.** If two levers move together, the result can't be read. Log each one in the ad name, run `/review`, and let `/learn` write the rule.
