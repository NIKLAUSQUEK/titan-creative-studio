- **Industry packs:** new `compliance-packs.md` sections "Media, publishers and community pages" + "Travel, tourism and experiences".
- **Market pack:** "Vietnam detail (verify)", added 2026-10-25, all marked verify.
- **The six rules that bite hardest:**
  1. **Label every paid or sponsored post.** Amended Advertising Law No. 75/2025/QH15, in force since 1 Jan 2026:
     - social media users must mark ads and sponsored content;
     - KOLs must check the advertiser and only promote what they have used or clearly understand;
     - ads must be disclosed before and during the promotion;
     - violating ads must come down within 24 h of a request from the authorities.

     Use Meta/TikTok paid-partnership tools plus "Quảng cáo / Sponsored" in the caption and on slide 1.

     Sources: https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnam-amends-key-aspects-of-advertising-law/ (2025-07-09) and https://vietnamnet.vn/en/online-influencers-face-tighter-rules-in-vietnam-s-ad-overhaul-2427991.html (read 2026-10-25). Fine amounts reported elsewhere are unverified.
  2. **Vietnamese comes first in ads.** Advertising Law 2012 Art. 18:
     - ad content must be in Vietnamese; brand names, slogans and trade names are exempt;
     - when a second language is used, it sits below the Vietnamese at no more than three-quarters of its size;
     - in video, the Vietnamese comes first.

     So every paid creative carries a Vietnamese headline with the English below it, or counsel confirms an exemption for English-only ads aimed at foreign tourists [verify]. Organic posts are a grey area [verify].

     Sources: https://lawnet.vn/en/laws/co-cac-phuong-tien-quang-cao-nao-tieng-noi-chu-viet-trong-quang-cao-duoc-quy-dinh-ra-sao-838077-76389.html and https://avia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Vietnam-2024-Advertising-Matrix-_Final.pdf (read 2026-10-25).
  3. **Scam exposés: patterns, not names.**
     - Never name, picture or tag a private person or business as a scammer unless an official source has done it first (police statement, court, or Tuoi Tre/VnExpress reporting citing police). Link that source.
     - Otherwise describe the pattern: "the rigged taxi meter", "the shoe-shine upsell".
     - Posting false or slanderous information that harms a person's or organisation's honour can be fined under Decree 15/2020 Art. 101 (as amended), with criminal slander liability on top.

     Source: https://www.tilleke.com/insights/new-penalties-posting-fake-news-social-networks/ (read 2026-10-25).
  4. **Blur faces and plates; get consent for images.**
     - Civil Code 2015 Art. 32 requires consent to use a person's image (paid if commercial), with only a narrow public-activity exception. The Advertising Law bars using someone's image in an ad without consent.
     - In scam, street and news content: blur bystanders, number plates, shop names and phone numbers unless they come from an official source.
     - Use customer shoot photos in ads only with written opt-in. The terms only grant an opt-out portfolio right, which is not ad consent.

     Source: https://lawnet.vn/thong-tin-phap-luat/en/tu-van-luat/is-the-use-of-other-people-s-images-without-permission-subject-to-compensation-in-vietnam-102840.html (read 2026-10-25).
  5. **Stay "tips", not "the press".**
     - The page is not a licensed newspaper. Never use "báo", "press" or "official" in its name or copy. Credit the outlet for news facts.
     - Avoid political, state and leader topics and anything about criticising the authorities. Social posts about such topics have drawn fines (https://www.rfa.org/english/news/vietnam/facebookers-fined-warned-posting-information-nguyen-phu-trong-07222024164456.html).
     - Since 25 Dec 2024, page admin accounts must be verified with a phone number under Decree 147/2024 (https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1666838/social-network-users-must-authenticate-accounts-from-december-25.html).
  6. **Offer truth and personal data.**
     - Prices, inclusions and the no-refund policy go in ads exactly as on the site. "Same day" and "next morning" are only claimed for the packages that promise them.
     - No fake scarcity.
     - No reviews until real ones exist, and never invent, buy or edit them. The happy-customers page is empty.
     - Lead forms and WhatsApp capture need explicit consent under the Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15, in force since 1 Jan 2026 (https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2025/06/vietnam-new-personal-data-protection-law.html).
- **Platform policy hot spots:**
  - Scam and crime content can trip Meta/TikTok "sensational" or "shocking" filters in ads. Run scam content organically, and keep the paid creative calm and practical.
  - Never imply the Vietnamese state endorses the brand.
  - No drone or flycam shots unless a permit is held [verify].
  - The Independence Palace charges commercial photographers VND 500,000 per camera from 1 Aug 2026, plus package fees, so leave it off the spot list or price the fee in (https://news.tuoitre.vn/who-has-to-pay-the-19-camera-fee-at-ho-chi-minh-citys-independence-palace-103260811125303134.htm).

## 9. Do / don't (hard rules; also copied into every spec.json)
- **Do:**
  - Put "Quảng cáo / Sponsored" plus the platform paid-partnership tag on every paid or partner post.
  - On paid ads, put the Vietnamese headline first, with the English below it at no more than 3/4 size, until counsel clears English-only.
  - Cite a source line on every news or scam slide (police, Tuoi Tre, VnExpress, or official tourism notices).
  - Blur faces, plates, shop signs and phone numbers in street and scam visuals.
  - Mark generated or recreated scenes "dramatisation / minh hoạ".
  - Quote prices, inclusions and the no-refund policy exactly as on the live site.
  - Use customer photos in ads only with a written ad opt-in.
- **Don't:**
  - Name or picture a private person or business as a scammer without an official source.
  - Run rage-bait or xenophobic framing about vendors, drivers or any nationality.
  - Use superlatives ("best", "safest", "#1").
  - Use "tour" or "official" (unless licensed).
  - Use fake or edited reviews, or any review at all until real ones exist.
  - Touch politics or state topics.
  - Use drone shots without a permit.
  - Use AI likeness of real people.
  - Use chart songs on brand or paid posts; licensed or original audio only.
