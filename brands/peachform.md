## 2. Market & rules
- Market(s) + language(s): Singapore, English with light Singlish; SEA (MY, ID, VN) later [draft] → locale pack: `localisation.md` 🇸🇬 SINGAPORE (light Singlish for brand ads, heavier for skits)
- Industry: fitness and body image, plus women's health/physio education → compliance packs: `compliance-packs.md` **Fitness, weight loss and body image** × **Singapore**, plus **Health, medical, allied health** wherever a physio or clinical claim appears, and **Beauty and cosmetics** if it ever sells shaping/"lifting" apparel claims.
- Rules that bite hardest:
  1. **No unrealistic transformations and no "grow your glutes in X weeks" claims.** No before/after miracle framing. Results talk = mechanism and training principles only (fitness pack; motion-design.md).
  2. **No superlatives** ("best", "#1", "most effective") and no comparisons with other coaches or brands (SG ASAS + universal pack).
  3. **Physio angle = education, not a clinical claim.** Klaus appears with his registered title only. No cure/treat claims (knee pain, back pain, pelvic floor). If PeachForm is tied to Physio Spot or heal360, the SG healthcare ad rules apply in full: no testimonials, no paid reviews, no free-consult or discount inducements.
  4. **Testimonials / UGC:** never fake them or imply typical results. Paid creators disclose (#ad / paid-partnership tool).
  5. **Body image and sensual edge:** the confident and flirty tone is allowed (mature-edge.md §2), fully clothed in gym or athleisure, adults clearly 25+, no nudity, no body-shaming, no zoomed-in body-part targeting in ads. Plan an **ad-safe cut** (Meta/TikTok) and an **organic edgy cut**.
  6. **Real people:** no AI likeness of real athletes or influencers. The cast is original characters (character-design.md) or consenting real talent.
- Platform policy hot spots: Meta and TikTok restrict "body-part focus" and before/after imagery in ads and may flag glute content as sexualised. TikTok bans weight-loss/body-transformation claims in ads. Meta limits ads that imply a viewer's body or personal attributes ("Struggling with flat glutes?" is a violation; "Build stronger glutes" is fine).
