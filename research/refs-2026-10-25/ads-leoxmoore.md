## 2. His argument (from the transcript)
1. Drama-style AI ads beat "generic slop". Example of slop he mocks: the talking-object AI trend ("Oh, I'm a tomato. This is what I do").
2. "If you make a … drama ad, you are going to do 10 times better than just making some generic slop" (an opinion, not a measured result: treat "10x" as rhetoric).
3. AI is fine as the production tool when the ad looks real: "it literally looks like it's shot in the office or something, but it's AI."
4. The structure is classic direct response hidden inside a scene: "it's two minutes in before the product gets introduced. And the promise is already there. There's already been so much objection handling. The outcome is clear."
5. Risk reversal plus urgency close it: "They have a 30-day money-back guarantee, so there's no risk. That's how confident they are." (He reads this from the ad.)

## 3. The ad he reviews (beat map)
Product: "Smooch Reverse Time Serum" (pink bottle), an anti-ageing serum. The setting is a DMV-style ID office with a counter, a number board and fluorescent light. AI-generated, photoreal.

| Ad beat | What happens (dialogue, verbatim where audible) | DR job Leo labels |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | Clerk: "Ma'am, I cannot accept this. The woman in this photo was 61." Woman: "I am 61." Clerk: "You are not 61." | Avatar call-out (a 61-year-old woman) and the hook in one line |
| Evidence | Clerk: "Look at the mouth in this photo. Those lines are not on your face." | Dream outcome shown as the problem |
| Escalation | "I can't verify this document." "Verify what? My own face?" "Ma'am, lower your voice." "Let me speak to your manager." | Conflict and stakes: she cannot get her ID |
| Second character | Manager/colleague: "Did you get Botox? Fillers? Threads? One of those laser treatments?" "No." "Then it must be a cream." | Objection handling: names every alternative and rules it out |
| Price anchor | "My sister spends $400 a jar on her serum, and she doesn't look anything like you." | Price anchoring against competitors |
| Failed solutions | "I've tried it all. Nothing did anything for over 10 years." | Objection handling: "nothing works for me" |
| Trade | "If I tell you, will you stamp it so I can leave?" "Stamp it." "Tell me." | Curiosity gap: the product is withheld as a bargaining chip |
| Reveal | She hands over the bottle: "This is it… wrinkles, fine lines… all in my formula." | Mechanism and product |
| Offer | "30-day money-back guarantee, so there's no risk" | Risk reversal |
| Close | "You may want to update your ID photo once you start using it." "Click Shop Now below." | Callback joke, urgency and CTA |

**Why it works (his reasons plus ours):**
- **The conflict is the claim.** The plot exists only because the product works ("you look too young to be you"). A sceptical stranger states the benefit, so the brand never says it.
- **Every objection is voiced by a character** (Botox? fillers? cream? price? "tried everything") before the product appears. The viewer's resistance is spent on the story.
- **The product is a reward.** It is withheld as a trade ("stamp it, then I'll tell you"), so the reveal lands as a payoff, not as an ad break.
- **It looks like content.** Real-world location, ordinary people, no branding until late. It reads as a scene from a show, which matches Leo's wider rule that ads must "merge into the feed" (see ad-strategy.md §2).
- **Simple, universal stakes.** Bureaucracy, "speak to your manager" and a stamp are global and need no setup.

## 4. What NOT to copy (compliance)
- In SG, a beauty/cosmetic ad that implies reversing age ("Reverse Time", "update your ID photo") needs cosmetic-only wording ("looks smoother"). A guarantee and a competitor price comparison need substantiation and real terms. For SG health brands (Physio Spot, heal360): no money-back or results guarantee, no price anchoring against other providers, and the "she looks too young = proof" device is an outcome testimonial. Use the structure, swap the payoff (see ad-strategy.md §8).
- The "10x" figure is Leo's opinion in a reel, not data.
- Study the structure only; never recut his reel or the Smooch ad into a deliverable (CLAUDE.md IP rule).
