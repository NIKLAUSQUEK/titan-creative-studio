**Full carousel slide copy**

*P1 (physio): "5 desk habits quietly loading your neck"*
1. Cover over a real physio photo.
2. "#1 happens every time you check your phone".
3. Screen too low → top of screen at eye level.
4. Laptop-only setup → add a stand and an external keyboard.
5. Long static holds → change position every 30–45 min.
6. Phone tilt → bring the phone up, not your head down.
7. Shallow breathing under stress → 3 slow breaths, shoulders down.
8. Recap grid.
9. "Pain that lasts, spreads or comes with numbness? See a qualified professional."
10. CTA: "Save for your next WFH day · Learn more about physiotherapy at [clinic]".

*P2 (physio): knee pain on stairs*
1. Cover.
2. "A common presentation (composite, educational)".
3. What we ask.
4. What we assess (real clinic photo).
5. What research generally supports: education plus progressive loading, with a guideline citation.
6. Progression ladder: sit-to-stand → step-ups → split squat.
7. "Timelines vary".
8. Myth: "Rest alone usually isn't the whole plan".
9. Sources.
10. CTA: save and share with a runner.

*PR1 (protein): myths*
- Myths: protein is only for bodybuilders · more is always better · plant protein can't work · shakes replace meals.
- Each myth gets its fact slide, citing dietary guidelines.
- Close: "Which surprised you?" plus a small product shot.

*PR2 (protein): 5 ways to hit 20 g in under 5 min*
- Real overhead photography.
- Re-hook: "#3 tastes like dessert".
- One recipe per slide, with grams verified from the label.
- Cheat-sheet slide.
- "Pair with whole foods".
- CTA: save and send to the gym chat.

*W1 (women's fitness): glute moves*
- Alternating ❌/✅ pairs: hip thrust (chin tuck, ribs down) · RDL (hinge, not squat) · lunge (front-foot pressure) · kickback (no back arch).
- Checklist.
- CTA: "Save for leg day · Follow for part 2".

*W2 (women's fitness): "Things nobody tells you when you start lifting"*
- Week 1: confused by machines.
- Week 2: DOMS on the stairs.
- Week 4: first weight added.
- Week 8: you look forward to leg day.
- "The real win: showing up 3×/week".
- Tip: start with 2–3 full-body sessions.
- CTA: "Send to the friend who keeps saying 'next month'".

*A1 (AI agency): automation checks*
- "Don't automate until it passes 4 checks".
- Diagram: Frequency (more than 20 times a week?) → Rules (can a new hire follow it from a checklist?) → Data (digital and clean?) → Risk (cost of a wrong output, and where is the human check?).
- Worked example: clinic appointment reminders.
- "Screenshot this".
- CTA: "Comment AUDIT for the scoring sheet".

*A2 (AI agency): admin before and after*
- "3 hours of admin before vs after automation (illustrative)".
- Anonymised screenshots.
- Task-by-task time comparison.
- Where humans stay in the loop.
- Chart labelled "example scenario".
- What to automate first.

*F1 (founder): "Month 3: S$__ in the bank and zero clients"*
- Real photo.
- One beat per slide (≤20 words).
- Lesson from a real story card.
- "If you're in month 3…".
- CTA: follow.

*F2 (founder): tweet-card thought leadership*
- Opening card: "Most small businesses don't need more content. They need 1 offer people understand."
- Argument cards.
- Counter-argument acknowledged.
- "Agree or disagree?"

**Template timings (15 / 60 / 180 s)**
Each template is listed as segment ranges in seconds, in the order 15 / 60 / 180.
- **Template 1 (ABT):**
  - 15 s: and 0–3, but 3–8, therefore 8–13, CTA 13–15
  - 60 s: 0–8, 8–30, 30–52, 52–60
  - 180 s: 0–20, 20–90, 90–165, 165–180
- **Template 2 (man-in-a-hole):**
  - 15 s: 0–3, 3–7, 7–12, 12–15
  - 60 s: 0–5, 5–25, 25–50, 50–60
  - 180 s: 0–15, 15–90, 90–160, 160–180
- **Template 3 (story spine):**
  - 15 s: 0–3, 3–6, 6–11, 11–14, 14–15
  - 60 s: 0–8, 8–14, 14–42, 42–54, 54–60
  - 180 s: 0–25, 25–40, 40–130, 130–165, 165–180
- **Template 4 (5-second moment):**
  - 15 s: 0–3, 3–7, 7–12, 12–15
  - 60 s: 0–5, 5–30, 30–50, 50–60
  - 180 s: 0–15, 15–100, 100–150, 150–180
- **Template 5 (Kishōtenketsu):**
  - 15 s: 0–4, 4–8, 8–12, 12–15
  - 60 s: 0–15, 15–30, 30–48, 48–60
  - 180 s: 0–40, 40–90, 90–140, 140–180
- **Template 6 (SB7):**
  - 60 s: hero + problem 0–15, guide 15–25, plan 25–40, failure/success 40–54, CTA 54–60
  - 180 s: 0–40, 40–70, 70–120, 120–165, 165–180
- **Templates 7–20:** scale proportionally from the 30 s column in the playbook. For 180 s, add a rehook at about 90 s.
